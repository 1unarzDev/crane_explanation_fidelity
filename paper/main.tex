\title{Beyond Terminal Events: Evidence-Checked Physical Diagnosis\\
for Natural-Language Robot Navigation Explanations}

\author{Anonymous submission}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Robot navigation systems expose action results, behavior-tree transitions, maps, commands, and
motion estimates, yet a fluent explanation can still stop at ``recovery was exhausted'' or invent
a physical cause that those signals do not establish. We present a diagnosis-to-language pipeline
that first computes a bounded physical or execution mechanism, links it to exact runtime and source
evidence, records conflicting evidence and unresolved alternatives, and only then realizes and
checks natural language. We distinguish diagnostic correctness from language faithfulness and
design a matched-evidence, matched-tool comparison against repository-aware agents. A separately
preserved legacy study evaluates runtime-to-source provenance and factual restraint but is not
reinterpreted as physical diagnosis. Development evidence exercises geometric restriction and
command-to-motion mechanisms in simulated land navigation and a focused surface-vessel setting,
including cases with deliberately missing decisive evidence. Across nine development clusters
(52 responses), the repository-aware baseline often matched the proposed diagnosis when given the
same tool, and every proposed final answer used deterministic fallback (13/13). A separately
predeclared, reference-audited Luna judge historically qualified on two fresh held-out passes. A
later prospective command--motion endpoint-threat extension failed its composite, core-field, and
protected gates, so semantic confirmation is blocked; no confirmatory response has been scored and
no alpha consumed. We therefore report no semantic method effect. This WIP establishes
reproducible diagnostic measurements and a bounded evaluation path, but not superiority.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The last message emitted by a navigation stack is rarely its most useful explanation. An action
may abort after recovery exhaustion, but a user or maintainer needs to know what prevented a
successful path or execution. Conversely, telemetry preceding an abort does not license an
unobserved obstacle identity, a unique actuator defect, or a causal claim about wind or waves.
Natural-language generation makes this tension acute: the most readable answer can be the least
auditable one.

We study explanations whose claims are constrained by retained robot evidence. As
Fig.~\ref{fig:pipeline} shows, the method separates robot-visible evidence, bounded diagnosis,
checked planning, realization, and final verification from evaluator-only truth and scoring.
The diagnosis is not free-form reasoning. It is a versioned computation over quantities with
units, frames, time intervals, evidence identifiers, assumptions, and applicability limits. Its
output identifies candidate mechanisms, supporting and contradictory observations, and
alternatives that remain observationally equivalent. The language layer must preserve this
content; if it cannot be verified after one bounded repair, the system falls back to a checked
deterministic rendering.

The paper asks three prospective questions. \emph{Q1:} do physical diagnostic computations plus
runtime/source provenance improve correct and informative failure explanations over a strong
repository-aware agent with equivalent evidence and tools? \emph{Q2:} does final language preserve
the diagnosis, decisive measurements, mechanism, and limitations? \emph{Q3:} does the method give
a mechanism when evidence supports one and narrow its answer when decisive evidence is absent?

This work makes three bounded contributions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a diagnosis record and checked language path that preserve measurement semantics,
  provenance, conflicts, unresolved alternatives, and permitted causal language;
  \item two navigation-focused diagnostics---geometric restriction and command-to-motion
  discrepancy---with explicit conditions under which they must abstain or qualify; and
  \item an audited WIP evaluation that separates deterministic diagnostic measurements from
  Luna-assessed semantic judgments, carries finite judge-error sensitivity into the planned
  comparison, and preserves negative baseline, verifier, and judge-development results.
\end{itemize}
We do not claim universal root-cause discovery, human trust, hardware reliability, or that
structured records alone are novel. The empirical scope is simulation-based navigation.

\section{Related Work}

Liu and Brand\~ao generate environment changes that restore feasibility to otherwise unsolved
motion-planning problems~\cite{liu2024environment}. Their work motivates geometric feasibility
checks, but a feasible reconstructed model does not establish successful physical execution, and
local free space does not prove global route absence. Diehl and Ramirez-Amaro learn an explicit
causal model and search for contrastive assignments associated with success~\cite{diehl2022fail}.
Their assumptions clarify why event order alone is not causal evidence.

REFLECT organizes multisensory histories into sensory, event, and subgoal levels to explain robot
failures~\cite{liu2023reflect}. Its reported limitations on low-level control motivate retaining
commands, actuation evidence, and measured motion rather than only scene summaries. HEXAR routes
questions to specialized component explainers and evaluates root-cause identification separately
from incorrect facts~\cite{love2026hexar}. We similarly separate useful diagnosis from unsupported
content, but additionally test whether diagnostic content survives language realization.

Our language path combines established, rather than individually novel, ideas. Explicit
plan-before-realization methods make content selection and ordering inspectable
~\cite{moryossef2019step}; atomic factual-precision evaluation motivates claim-level support but
does not measure whether a decisive mechanism was omitted~\cite{min2023factscore}; and
bidirectional semantic checks test both omissions and unsupported additions after realization
~\cite{dusek2020semantic}. Selective prediction motivates reporting error jointly with useful
coverage~\cite{geifman2019selectivenet}. Explainability-by-Design already connects provenance,
queries, explanation plans, and realization~\cite{huynh2023explainability}. Our narrower
contribution is a robot-navigation evaluation in which the intermediate object is a validated,
bounded physical or execution diagnosis, and diagnostic correctness is scored separately from
language faithfulness. We do not claim priority for structured plans, factual checking,
abstention, or provenance-backed generation.

Marine motion illustrates the need for semantic restraint: propulsion, current-relative
hydrodynamics, wind, and wave loads are distinct terms in standard craft models~\cite{fossen2021handbook}.
A lateral residual therefore does not uniquely establish ``wave drift.'' More generally, a
navigation feedback field may not mean what its label suggests. In the audited Nav2 Jazzy source,
\texttt{FollowPath.feedback.speed} is computed from the commanded velocity rather than independent
motion~\cite{nav2controller}. Our diagnostic contracts retain command and measured motion as
different evidence types.

\section{Evidence and Diagnostic Method}

\subsection{Evidence boundary}

Robot-visible inputs and evaluator-only truth are physically separated. Robot-visible evidence may
include goals, paths, action/behavior-tree traces, exact source and configuration artifacts,
delivered scans or costmaps, commands, accepted actuation where available, and independently
measured motion. Evaluator truth contains injected conditions, canonical simulator geometry,
hidden force decomposition, expected propositions, and answerability labels. A diagnostic sensor
need not have been consumed by Nav2 to establish a physical condition. Claiming that Nav2 chose an
action \emph{because} it consumed that observation requires a recorded runtime dependency.

We retain four evidence levels: recorded sequence; reconstructed software mechanism; inference
under an explicit model; and controlled-intervention result. Temporal order alone supports only
the first level. Every claim names its evidence IDs and level.

\subsection{Diagnostic result}

A diagnostic result stores the episode and interval; input hashes; measurements with units,
frames, and timestamps; computation name and version; parameters and thresholds; assumptions and
applicability; supporting and contradictory evidence; distinguishable and unresolved alternatives;
the permitted causal-language level; and one fixed-menu next check. Evidence sufficiency is labeled
independently of whether our implementation finds the desired mechanism.

\subsection{Geometric restriction}

The geometric diagnostic compares observed free space with the actual footprint at orientation,
configured safety envelope, and requested path. It reports physical and configured-envelope
clearance separately. A disconnected audited grid can establish no traversable connection only
within that grid, resolution, threshold, and footprint model. Planner failure without such a check
is not proof of infeasibility. When interventions are used, the system executes a new run from a
controlled initial condition; recorded-state playback is not called a counterfactual execution.

\subsection{Command-to-motion discrepancy}

The execution diagnostic aligns desired commands, accepted/applied commands where available,
actuator feedback, and independent pose or velocity. Healthy response bounds are calibrated on
development trials and include delay and inertia. A discrepancy supports an execution-response
mechanism but does not uniquely identify slip, collision, motor failure, or environmental loading.

One concrete terminal-margin check computes
\begin{align*}
m_{\mathrm{return}} &= \tau_{\mathrm{task}} - e_{\mathrm{return}},\\
\Delta e_{\mathrm{settle}} &= e_{\mathrm{settled}} - e_{\mathrm{return}},
\end{align*}
where $\tau_{\mathrm{task}}$ is the declared task tolerance and $e$ is independently measured goal
error. If the action returns under its configured pose and stopped-speed thresholds, but
$\Delta e_{\mathrm{settle}} > m_{\mathrm{return}}$ and the platform settles outside the task
tolerance, the evidence supports an inadequate terminal stopping margin. It does not establish why
the residual motion occurred.

\subsection{Checked answer and final verification}

Answers contain four short sections: \emph{Diagnosis}; \emph{Decisive evidence}; \emph{Failure
chain}; and \emph{Limits and next check}. Measurements are included only when they support the
mechanism. The diagnostic plan is checked before generation, but the final text is also checked.
Unverified clauses are never silently accepted. We retain candidate text, verification outcome,
repair/fallback decision, final text, latency, tokens, and cost where available.

\section{Evaluation Design}

\subsection{Separate legacy evidence set}

Before the redirect, we froze a paired land/Nav2 provenance study over recovery-success and
terminal-abort episodes. Its questions, prompts, models, verifier, rubric, split, and inclusion
rules remain unchanged. Collection was prospectively closed at 33 included episode clusters (17
recovery-success and 16 terminal-abort), below the frozen minimum of 40 and target of 50. The
cohort contains 198 one-shot responses across three conditions. No sealed label or effect estimate
was inspected when collection was closed. The cohort remains available for analysis under its
original rubric, but is reported here only as under-target and unscored; it is not pooled with or
rescored as the diagnostic study.

The legacy study evaluates software-mechanism provenance, factual restraint, and evidence
insufficiency. Its checked condition used deterministic fallback for 46 of 66 responses (69.7\%),
including every recovery-mechanism response. We therefore report candidate and final-output
results separately and describe this retained operating point as predominantly deterministic.

\subsection{Prospective diagnostic comparison}

The planned prospective study compares four compact conditions. R is a strong repository-aware agent with the same
robot-visible evidence, exact source/configuration, and the same diagnostic tools and descriptions
available to the proposal. P receives validated diagnostic results plus provenance, checked
planning, generation, and final verification. T deterministically renders the same diagnostic
result. N is a new-arm provenance/checking ablation without physical diagnostic computations; it
does not modify the archived legacy method.

The primary endpoint is \emph{supported diagnostic success} on independently labeled diagnosable
questions: the final answer identifies the required mechanism and contains no material unsupported
or contradicted assertion. Secondary outcomes are diagnostic correctness, language faithfulness,
material-error risk, causal overclaiming, unnecessary abstention, appropriate qualification,
mechanism/location/time identification, evidence coverage and citation correctness, selected next
check, latency/cost, and fallback frequency.

Scenario families contain nominal success, successful compensation, similar terminal symptoms
from different mechanisms, the same mechanism with different outcomes, irrelevant obstacles,
sufficient versus missing evidence, false premises, and out-of-model causes. Shared layouts,
interventions, evidence masks, and question variants stay in one split and one statistical cluster.
Valid unexpected outcomes are retained; induction success and recording validity are reported
separately. Development pilots determine thresholds and inform a new clustered paired power
simulation before questions, labels, prompts, models, stopping rules, and held-out splits freeze.

The operational semantic arm was designed to use two context-isolated passes of GPT-6 Luna over
randomized, condition-blind packets, with condition keys withheld until scoring. These are repeated
model measurements, not two humans. Independent reference computations, not the runtime verifier
or checked plan, provide diagnostic reference units. A bounded development and held-out judge
qualification precedes study scoring; category errors, unresolved fields, pass disagreement, and
sensitivity bounds must accompany scenario-clustered paired estimates. Human packets remain
available for a later audit but are not a prerequisite for the automated arm.

Earlier v4--v6 judge configurations failed their prospectively fixed gates and remain ineligible.
The reference-audited v7 cycle retained the same Luna alias, high reasoning setting, prompt,
schema, isolation, two-pass rule, and thresholds over 24 fresh cases per pass. Both passes classified
20/20 composite endpoints and 32/32 required units correctly, with 176/187 and 179/187 core fields,
zero observed false rejections among 17 factual answers, zero observed false acceptances among six
unsupported answers, and no protected-test failures. That historical v7 result remains preserved.
Zero observed errors do not establish zero population error:
the upward-rounded Wilson 95\% upper bounds are 0.1844 for false rejection and 0.3904 for false
acceptance, and amendment~2's original bounds remain authoritative where applicable.

Before candidate-v3 activation, a prospectively frozen endpoint-threat extension applied the
unchanged judge to 24 new command--motion cases in each of two isolated passes. All 48 calls
completed, but composite accuracy was 16/20 and 17/20, core-field accuracy was 162/192 and
167/192, and both passes failed protected prompt-injection/boundary gates. Under the frozen hard
stop, the extension's smaller bounds are ineligible and Luna-scored semantic confirmation is
blocked. This later failure does not retroactively alter the historical v7 labels; it prevents
their use to activate the intended command--motion endpoint. No confirmatory response has been
scored and no alpha was consumed.

\section{Development Case: Terminal Margin}

We first exercised the complete path on a retained RoboBoat development run; it is not a held-out
result. Nav2 returned success at 0.3738~m goal error while independent odometry measured
0.04861~m/s. The exact configuration allowed 0.400~m XY error and 0.050~m/s stopped speed. Against
the declared 0.400~m task tolerance, only 0.0262~m margin remained. The vessel then moved 0.1876~m
and ended at 0.5614~m error. The checked answer therefore leads with an inadequate terminal margin,
connects the configured success to post-result motion, and withholds a unique claim about motor,
hydrodynamic, wind, current, wave, or collision causation.

A parity-audited development comparison gave R the same executable terminal-margin computation
and exact source/configuration as P. R, P/T, and even N expressed the central unmasked mechanism;
this one case provides no evidence that P improves on a strong tool-enabled agent. P's fluent
candidate failed the exact final-text check, so final P fell back to T.

A paired mask removed only measured speed at return. The original P/T plan then withheld the whole
mechanism, while R and N retained the still-supported positional-margin and post-return-motion
chain and withheld stopped-speed compliance. We retained this omission and corrected the plan only
for future use. The mask adds neither an independent episode nor a prospective result.

\section{Development Case: Geometric Restriction}

A current-source land run exercises the geometric path using only a delivered Nav2 global
costmap, delivered odometry, action timing, and exact configuration/source hashes. The requested
18~m centerline first intersects a cost-253 cell near $x=8.45$~m and has zero minimum clearance
from a lethal cell. The retained configuration uses a 0.22~m robot radius and 0.55~m inflation.
The robot moved as far as $x=6.949$~m, deviated 2.705~m laterally, and 69 planning updates returned
success before the action aborted in 70.860~s under an exact 70~s behavior-tree deadline.

Crucially, a separately versioned deterministic costmap audit finds that the retained grid still connects the
action-result pose to the goal below cost 253. The checked answer therefore reports a direct-route
restriction, substantial detour, and deadline-aligned abort; it does not claim global no-path,
name the evaluator-only obstacle, or attribute the result to recovery exhaustion. The terminal
behavior-tree transition was not delivered, so source semantics plus timing reconstruct the
deadline mechanism but do not directly observe its final tick. This development case is retained
as partial fault induction rather than tuned or discarded. It tests response usefulness and
causal restraint, not comparative effectiveness.

The first information-parity development comparison produced a useful negative result. Once R
received the same executable geometric audit as P, R communicated the same route restriction,
connected-grid qualification, detour, deadline alignment, and limits as P/T; this single case gives
no evidence that P outperforms a tool-enabled repository agent. P's fluent candidate was
substantively faithful but failed the conservative exact-text check, so its final answer fell back
to T. In these initial cases, final P was therefore a deterministic method, not evidence that
language realization improved the response.

Three further geometric checks delimit this result. Removing costmap cells made every method
withhold the physical restriction, but P/T initially also omitted the still-supported deadline
alignment. An independently recomputed S-turn case reproduced a direct-route intersection,
retained-grid connectivity, 1.699~m lateral deviation, and abort 0.918~s from the same deadline; R
again matched P/T on project review. A predeclared nominal control succeeded with complete route
coverage, no cost-253 intersection, and only 0.084~m maximum lateral deviation. These masks and
controls exposed false-premise and evidence-coverage bugs, which were retained and corrected only
for future use.

Two additional records exercise execution provenance without claiming physical causation. A
warehouse success establishes at least four source-qualified recovery invocations after recorded
planner-branch failures, but not their physical cause or whole-history completeness. A no-retry
instrumentation run retained 12,975 poses across 70 unique delivered plans; an independent
implementation reproduced all hashes and deviations, including a direct first plan and later
$-1.175$ to $+1.035$~m deviations. In both, tool-enabled R matched the central diagnosis on project
review, and P fell back to T. Separately, a later scenario-binding-qualified route-change record
was scored as a bounded development check under the then-qualified Luna v7 arm: both isolated passes marked P,
R, and T successful and N unsuccessful, yielding P-minus-R 0.0 in each pass. P and T were
byte-identical, yet one pass assigned them different secondary full/partial dispositions; we
retain that judge instability and do not seek a third vote. The warehouse and earlier
instrumentation records remain unscored development observations.

\section{Development Case: Command--Motion Discrepancy}

We next qualified synchronized command and independently delivered odometry evidence on a
predeclared held-condition/nominal development pair. The blind held-condition export contains 376
command samples and 1,598 goal-interval odometry samples. A fixed-window computation, reproduced
by a separate evaluator implementation, calibrates a healthy median measured response of
0.2597~m/s and identifies the earliest sustained discrepancy from 7--17~s: median delivered
command remains 0.800~m/s while measured planar motion is 0.000~m/s. The retained execution then
records two \texttt{FollowPath} failures, two source-qualified \texttt{Wait} invocations, a third
\texttt{FollowPath} attempt, and action abort.

This evidence supports an execution-level command--motion discrepancy connected to failure of
continued progress. It does not prove downstream actuator acceptance, Nav2 consumption of the
odometry samples, or a unique motor, slip, collision, obstruction, or intervention cause. The
matched nominal run uses the same thresholds, succeeds after 9.480~m, records neither
\texttt{FollowPath} failure nor source-qualified \texttt{Wait}, and correctly returns
\texttt{not\_triggered}. Thus the checked answer rejects rather than merely qualifies the false
failure premise.

A separately declared compensation case produced the same independently reproduced 8--18~s
discrepancy, then recovered 0.2597~m/s response at 20--21~s and eventually succeeded. Tool-enabled R
reported a wrong 8--20~s interval on project review, while raw P preserved the checked timing but
again fell back under the operating verifier. This is a plausible differentiation case, not a
qualified label. Conversely, removing all independent odometry caused every method to withhold the
discrepancy; the execution sequence remained answerable. These cases test outcome independence and
appropriate abstention without adding independent evidence through masks.

The supported, nominal, and missing-odometry packets contain 12 blinded responses but form one
paired/masked cluster. The compensated run adds one distinct four-response development cluster.
The delivered-plan run adds one distinct four-response cluster. The complete diagnostic development
inventory therefore contains 52 responses in 13 packets over nine clusters. Its original inventory
remains unscored; separately governed later development labels do not turn it into a held-out
result. Immutable final P fallback is 13/13 development questions overall.

A separate frozen land command--motion physical/reference cohort now contains sixteen valid,
single-attempt configurations from its fixed 100-configuration confirmation schedule. It has no
method responses or semantic labels. In run 003, the predeclared odometry mask removes the decisive
motion measurement while preserving execution facts, so the independent method reference withholds
the discrepancy mechanism. In run 004, independently recomputed measured motion recovered after
the discrepancy, but the navigation action later aborted. The latter prevents equating recovered
response with task success. Run 005 supplies a successful nominal \texttt{not\_triggered} control.
Run 006 independently repeats the missing-odometry boundary while retaining an unexpected abort;
run 007 adds a fresh-layout nominal control. Run 008 adds independently reproduced response loss,
recovery, and eventual task success. Runs 009 and 010 supply persistent-discrepancy cases on both
geometry strata, with independently reproduced response loss followed by action abort and no
unique physical-cause claim.
Run 011 adds another independently reproduced response recovery followed by task success.
Run 012 adds a nominal case where recovery activity occurred but the measured discrepancy did not.
Run 013 adds a clean single-attempt nominal case without recovery activity.
Run 014 adds another persistent discrepancy followed by action abort.
Run 015 adds another measured-response recovery followed by task success.
Run 016 adds the corresponding nominal-clear-route compensation case.
These are physical/reference observations, not a semantic P--R effect.

The artifact proves that the retained measurements can be recomputed and rendered into bounded
diagnoses. It does not prove that P improves supported diagnostic success over R. Project review

so turning the observations into confirmatory counts, risk--coverage curves, or significance tests
would overstate the evidence.

\section{Limitations and Validity}

The diagnostic families are deliberately narrow. A correct result applies only to its retained
model, signals, interval, and assumptions. Delivered observations do not prove controller
consumption; local geometry does not prove global infeasibility; a command--motion residual does
not uniquely identify a physical source. Evaluator truth is unavailable to ordinary methods.

Experiments use simulation. They can test controlled mechanisms and simulation-based ecological
relevance, not hardware reliability or real-world transfer. The study measures factual and
diagnostic outcomes, not human trust. The original Luna v7 arm passed both fresh held-out
qualification passes, but its later targeted endpoint-threat extension failed both passes and no
confirmatory response has been scored. Repeated-model measurements would not constitute human
agreement. The failed composition audit further shows that judge qualification cannot repair
an incomplete case reference. Its small error-category denominators also yield large finite-sample sensitivity
bounds that a future nominal advantage may fail. The legacy cohort stopped below its
frozen minimum and has no completed semantic scoring, so it supports neither an effect estimate nor
a significance claim. The development cases were selected to exercise the pipeline; only a
separately frozen prospective study can support comparative claims.

Finally, deterministic rendering may outperform or dominate language-model realization. At this
development operating point it supplied every final P answer; without qualified semantic labels we
report that fallback frequency but do not infer comparative quality or attribute templated final
answers to language-model reasoning.

\section{Conclusion}

Useful robot explanations require more than narrating the event that ended an action. Our method
places a validated, bounded diagnosis between robot evidence and language, then checks whether the
final response preserves both the mechanism and its limits. Development evidence shows that the
measurements and qualifications are reproducible, but also that a fair tool-enabled agent often
matches the diagnosis, conservative verification can erase faithful language, and automated-judge
qualification requires complete independent references and explicit error sensitivity. We therefore make no superiority claim. The WIP artifact instead
establishes an auditable basis for a later clustered comparison while preserving the distinction
between observation, reconstructed software semantics, modeled inference, and intervention.
