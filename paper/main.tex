\title{How Specific Should a Robot Explanation Be?\\
Evidence-Calibrated Failure Diagnosis under Partial Observability}

\author{Anonymous submission}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
Natural-language robot explanations can be physically correct yet unjustified by the evidence
available to the robot. We study \emph{evidence-calibrated specificity}: whether an explanation
asserts the deepest diagnostic mechanism supported by robot-visible evidence, becomes less
specific when decisive evidence is removed, and retains useful partial diagnoses instead of
silently overclaiming or over-abstaining. We formalize explanations as atomic claims with explicit
evidence requirements and non-entailments, and propose controlled, nested evidence interventions
over autonomous-navigation episodes. The resulting benchmark separates evaluator-only physical
truth from method-visible evidence and measures unsupported specificity, supported diagnostic
recall, evidence-level calibration, monotonicity, abstention, and false-premise rejection. Our
full method computes a maximal supported diagnostic plan, realizes it in language, and verifies
final clauses against approved claim identifiers and values. Strong raw, structured-evidence, and
tool-enabled agent baselines receive the same permitted evidence; the intended contribution is
safer calibration at useful coverage, not universally better root-cause diagnosis. Preserved
development evidence motivates this redirect: a strong tool-enabled agent often matched the
central diagnosis, all 13 original proposed answers used deterministic fallback, and multiple
candidate and automated-judge campaigns were negative, inconclusive, or invalid for confirmatory
inference. We report those outcomes as development evidence and make no statistical superiority,
human-trust, hardware-transfer, or model-faithfulness claim.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The last message emitted by a navigation stack is rarely its most useful explanation. An action
may abort after recovery exhaustion, but a user or maintainer needs to know what prevented a
successful path or execution. Conversely, telemetry preceding an abort does not license an
unobserved obstacle identity, a unique actuator defect, or a causal claim about wind or waves.
Natural-language generation makes this tension acute: a plausible answer can be physically true
in the simulator yet unjustified by anything the robot was permitted to observe.

We ask whether explanation specificity tracks the evidence actually available to the robot. As
Fig.~\ref{fig:pipeline} shows, the method separates robot-visible evidence, claim-contract
evaluation, maximal-supported diagnosis, realization, and final verification from evaluator-only
physical truth and scoring. Each atomic claim has required evidence and explicit non-entailments.
The strongest valid output may therefore be a partial diagnosis, an ambiguity set, an
insufficient-evidence statement, or rejection of a false failure premise. The objective is not
silence: a calibrated system should retain every useful supported claim while withholding only
the unsupported increase in causal or mechanistic specificity.

The paper asks three prospective questions. \emph{Q1:} when decisive evidence is absent, do
explanation systems avoid claims whose evidential requirements are unsatisfied? \emph{Q2:} do they
retain the deepest useful partial diagnosis supported by each evidence condition rather than
over-abstaining? \emph{Q3:} across nested evidence conditions, does asserted specificity increase
only when the added evidence supports that increase, including on false-premise success controls?

This work makes three bounded contributions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a formalization of evidence-calibrated robotic explanation using atomic claims,
  evidence requirements, diagnostic abstraction levels, non-entailments, partial diagnoses,
  ambiguity, false-premise handling, and evidence monotonicity;
  \item a controlled navigation benchmark in which deterministic nested evidence interventions
  vary robot-visible support while evaluator-only physical truth, episode identity, and
  provenance remain fixed; and
  \item a diagnostic-contract method and comparative risk--coverage evaluation that test whether
  enforcing maximal supported specificity reduces overclaiming without collapsing useful
  diagnostic coverage.
\end{itemize}
These are exactly the three claimed contributions. We do not claim universal root-cause
discovery, improved human trust, hardware reliability, model-internal faithfulness, or that
structured records alone are novel. The empirical scope is simulation-based land navigation.

\section{Related Work}

Liu and Brand\~ao generate environment changes that restore feasibility to otherwise unsolved
motion-planning problems~\cite{liu2024environment}. Their work motivates geometric feasibility
checks, but a feasible reconstructed model does not establish successful physical execution, and
local free space does not prove global route absence. Diehl and Ramirez-Amaro learn an explicit
causal model and search for contrastive assignments associated with success~\cite{diehl2022fail}.
Their assumptions clarify why event order alone is not causal evidence.

REFLECT organizes multisensory histories into sensory, event, and subgoal levels to explain robot
failures~\cite{liu2023reflect}. Its reported limitations on low-level control motivate retaining
commands, actuation evidence, and measured motion rather than only scene summaries. HEXAR routes
questions to specialized component explainers and evaluates root-cause identification separately
from incorrect facts~\cite{love2026hexar}. We similarly separate useful diagnosis from unsupported
content, but additionally test whether diagnostic content survives language realization.

Our language path combines established, rather than individually novel, ideas. Explicit
plan-before-realization methods make content selection and ordering inspectable
~\cite{moryossef2019step}; atomic factual-precision evaluation motivates claim-level support but
does not measure whether a decisive mechanism was omitted~\cite{min2023factscore}; and
bidirectional semantic checks test both omissions and unsupported additions after realization
~\cite{dusek2020semantic}. Selective prediction motivates reporting error jointly with useful
coverage~\cite{geifman2019selectivenet}. Explainability-by-Design already connects provenance,
queries, explanation plans, and realization~\cite{huynh2023explainability}. Fern\'andez-Becerra
et al. further combine curated ROS~2 navigation events with agentic RAG, retrieval and
hallucination graders, and natural-language explanation evaluation~\cite{fernandezbecerra2026rag};
their accountable black-box design supplies textual logs and visual context to LLM/VLM
explainers~\cite{fernandezbecerra2026accountability}. We therefore claim no priority for ROS-log
RAG, accountable recording, source-aware retrieval, multimodal context, or hallucination grading.
Our narrower contribution is to test whether independently computed measurements establish a
bounded physical or execution mechanism---with conflicts, unresolved alternatives, and explicit
evidence sufficiency---and whether final language asserts exactly the supported level. Diagnostic
correctness, claim support, and required-content coverage are scored separately.

Marine motion illustrates the need for semantic restraint: propulsion, current-relative
hydrodynamics, wind, and wave loads are distinct terms in standard craft models~\cite{fossen2021handbook}.
A lateral residual therefore does not uniquely establish ``wave drift.'' More generally, a
navigation feedback field may not mean what its label suggests. In the audited Nav2 Jazzy source,
\texttt{FollowPath.feedback.speed} is computed from the commanded velocity rather than independent
motion~\cite{nav2controller}. Our diagnostic contracts retain command and measured motion as
different evidence types.

\section{Evidence Calibration and Diagnostic Method}

\subsection{Terminology and formal objective}

Let $E$ denote the robot-visible evidence supplied under one deterministic evidence condition and
let $T$ denote evaluator-only physical truth. An atomic explanation claim $c$ is evidentially
supported only if its versioned requirement $\operatorname{RequiredEvidence}(c)$ is satisfied by
$E$. Physical truth ($c$ agrees with $T$) and evidential support are deliberately distinct: a
physically true claim is still an unsupported specificity error when its required evidence is
absent from $E$. \emph{Diagnostic correctness} asks whether the emitted diagnosis matches the
strongest supported mechanism; \emph{evidence calibration} asks whether the explanation's
specificity corresponds to the support available in $E$. We reserve \emph{model faithfulness} for
correspondence with a model's internal decision process, which this study does not test.

Diagnostic nodes form a mechanism-specific hierarchy or directed acyclic graph from task outcome
through software/action failure, recovery, command--motion or geometry evidence, bounded physical
execution mechanisms, and specific physical causes. For a condition $E$, the method validates
evidence, removes contradicted nodes, and selects the maximal supported node or incomparable set of
nodes. Its state is one of \texttt{KNOWN}, \texttt{SUPPORTED\_PARTIAL}, \texttt{AMBIGUOUS},
\texttt{INSUFFICIENT\_EVIDENCE}, \texttt{FALSE\_PREMISE}, or \texttt{NOT\_TRIGGERED}; these states
are not collapsed into a generic unknown. Appropriate abstention withholds only the unsupported
deeper mechanism, whereas over-abstention discards a useful supported node.

For nested conditions $E_0\subset E_1\subset\cdots\subset E_{\mathrm{k}}$, evidence monotonicity forbids an
answer under $E_{\mathrm{i}}$ from asserting a claim whose required evidence first appears only in some
$E_{\mathrm{j}}$, $\mathrm{j}>\mathrm{i}$. This constraint concerns evidential license, not verbal consistency: added evidence
may contradict an earlier candidate or resolve ambiguity. Each claim contract therefore records
required and optional evidence, supported propositions, forbidden implications, evidence
references, numeric values and tolerances, and required limitations. It never obtains an
episode-specific answer from evaluator-only truth.

\subsection{Evidence boundary}

Robot-visible inputs and evaluator-only truth are physically separated. Robot-visible evidence may
include goals, paths, action/behavior-tree traces, exact source and configuration artifacts,
delivered scans or costmaps, commands, accepted actuation where available, and independently
measured motion. Evaluator truth contains injected conditions, canonical simulator geometry,
hidden force decomposition, expected propositions, and answerability labels. A diagnostic sensor
need not have been consumed by Nav2 to establish a physical condition. Claiming that Nav2 chose an
action \emph{because} it consumed that observation requires a recorded runtime dependency. The
same boundary applies to images: seeing an obstacle can establish visual presence under the sensor
contract, but not Nav2 consumption or obstacle causation.

We retain four evidence levels: recorded sequence; reconstructed software mechanism; inference
under an explicit model; and controlled-intervention result. Temporal order alone supports only
the first level. Every claim names its evidence IDs and level. Deterministic masks only remove
fields or streams: they never rewrite values, events, or outcomes. Method-visible packets must
therefore be reproducible from the episode and mask identity while excluding every evaluator-only
field.

\subsection{Diagnostic result}

A diagnostic result stores the episode and interval; input hashes; measurements with units,
frames, and timestamps; computation name and version; parameters and thresholds; assumptions and
applicability; supporting and contradictory evidence; distinguishable and unresolved alternatives;
the permitted causal-language level; and one fixed-menu next check. Evidence sufficiency is labeled
independently of whether our implementation finds the desired mechanism.

\subsection{Geometric restriction}

The geometric diagnostic compares observed free space with the actual footprint at orientation,
configured safety envelope, and requested path. It reports physical and configured-envelope
clearance separately. A disconnected audited grid can establish no traversable connection only
within that grid, resolution, threshold, and footprint model. Planner failure without such a check
is not proof of infeasibility. When interventions are used, the system executes a new run from a
controlled initial condition; recorded-state playback is not called a counterfactual execution.

\subsection{Command-to-motion discrepancy}

The execution diagnostic aligns desired commands, accepted/applied commands where available,
actuator feedback, and independent pose or velocity. Healthy response bounds are calibrated on
development trials and include delay and inertia. A discrepancy supports an execution-response
mechanism but does not uniquely identify slip, collision, motor failure, or environmental loading.

One concrete terminal-margin check computes
\begin{align*}
m_{\mathrm{return}} &= \tau_{\mathrm{task}} - e_{\mathrm{return}},\\
\Delta e_{\mathrm{settle}} &= e_{\mathrm{settled}} - e_{\mathrm{return}},
\end{align*}
where $\tau_{\mathrm{task}}$ is the declared task tolerance and $e$ is independently measured goal
error. If the action returns under its configured pose and stopped-speed thresholds, but
$\Delta e_{\mathrm{settle}} > m_{\mathrm{return}}$ and the platform settles outside the task
tolerance, the evidence supports an inadequate terminal stopping margin. It does not establish why
the residual motion occurred.

\subsection{Claim-aware realization and final verification}

The diagnostic plan supplies approved claim identifiers, numeric values, support references,
required limitations, and relevant non-entailments. Realization maps each sentence or clause back
to those identifiers. The final verifier checks required-claim coverage, rejects unapproved
mechanistic claims, checks numeric values within declared tolerances, and preserves limitations
and false-premise status. A failed clause is removed or generalized to a supported ancestor; it
does not erase independently supported content. A deterministic claim-level reconstruction is the
fail-closed output when free language cannot be verified. We retain candidate text, clause maps,
verification decisions, final text, latency, tokens, and cost where available.

\section{Evaluation Design}

\subsection{Separate legacy evidence set}

Before the redirect, we froze a paired land/Nav2 provenance study over recovery-success and
terminal-abort episodes. Its questions, prompts, models, verifier, rubric, split, and inclusion
rules remain unchanged. Collection was prospectively closed at 33 included episode clusters (17
recovery-success and 16 terminal-abort), below the frozen minimum of 40 and target of 50. The
cohort contains 198 one-shot responses across three conditions. No sealed label or effect estimate
was inspected when collection was closed. The cohort remains available for analysis under its
original rubric, but is labeled \textsc{Legacy Exploratory / Under-Target} and reported only as
under-target and unscored; it is not pooled with or rescored as the evidence-calibration study.

The legacy study evaluates software-mechanism provenance, factual restraint, and evidence
insufficiency. Its checked condition used deterministic fallback for 46 of 66 responses (69.7\%),
including every recovery-mechanism response. We therefore report candidate and final-output
results separately and describe this retained operating point as predominantly deterministic.

\subsection{Prospective evidence-intervention comparison}

The prospective benchmark compares five logically distinct conditions. B0 receives the ordinary
deterministic runtime presentation without diagnostic contracts or executable diagnostic tools.
B1 receives structured robot-visible evidence but no executable contracts. B2 is the strongest
fair repository-aware, tool-enabled agent condition available. B3 runs the deterministic
diagnostic contract and planner but omits complete final-claim verification. B4 adds maximal-
supported planning, claim-ID-aware realization, and final removal or generalization of unsupported
clauses. A deterministic rendering is retained to separate diagnostic computation from language
generation. All paired conditions receive the same evidence condition and governed source and
configuration access appropriate to their declared interface; B2 is not intentionally weakened.

The primary paired comparison is B4 versus B2. For each independently generated episode, a method
incurs an evidence-calibration failure if, anywhere on its prospectively specified evidence
ladder, it (a) emits at least one mechanistic claim whose required evidence is absent from that
condition or (b) asserts a diagnostic abstraction level deeper than the evaluator-defined maximum
justified level. The confirmatory question is whether B4 reduces this episode-level failure risk
while retaining a development-selected, prospectively frozen minimum of useful diagnostic
coverage. The coverage floor is a precondition on interpretation, not a value chosen after
confirmatory scoring.

Secondary decompositions report unsupported specificity rate (unsupported mechanistic claims over
all emitted mechanistic claims), supported diagnostic recall (emitted required units over
available supported required units), exact evidence-calibration accuracy, over- and under-
specific errors, monotonicity violations, appropriate abstention, over-abstention, false-premise
rejection, and risk--coverage operating points. Every numerator and denominator is retained. No
single arbitrary aggregate replaces these quantities.

The independent unit is the prospectively generated episode/configuration. Evidence masks,
questions, prompts, methods, outputs, and annotations within an episode are clustered repeated
measurements. The primary analysis reports paired episode counts, discordant pairs, absolute risk
difference with interval, and an exact McNemar test where its assumptions apply. Evidence-level
analysis uses a predeclared episode-clustered model and whole-episode bootstrap intervals;
secondary multiplicity uses Holm correction. Power simulation is driven by plausible development
ranges for discordant episode probabilities, within-episode dependence, family prevalence, and
invalid-episode rate---never by treating masks as independent samples.
The current sensitivity uses the audited maximum remaining discovery alpha of 0.01. Under
hypothetical 8-, 16-, and 26-point paired reductions, 100 acquired episodes provide estimated
powers of 0.103, 0.500, and 0.923, respectively; the 16-point scenario first exceeds 0.80 at 200
episodes in the simulated grid. These values are planning assumptions, not effect estimates, and
no sample size is frozen.

The mature command--motion family is primary, with nominal response, persistent discrepancy,
transient discrepancy followed by recovery, abort after discrepancy, and invalid or absent
odometry. Geometry/planning and execution/recovery supply bounded secondary families where their
evidence contracts are stable. Each episode has deterministic nested evidence conditions that
remove outcome, recovery, commands, synchronized odometry, or stronger validated physical evidence
according to its frozen family ladder. Nominal successes, missing-evidence cases, ambiguity, and
false-premise questions are fixed controls. Shared layouts, interventions, masks, and paraphrases
remain in one split and one statistical cluster.

The prospective measurement uses two separately invoked, blinded automated agents over atomic-claim
packets. They classify each claim as supported, contradicted, insufficiently supported, physically
true but unsupported, or uninterpretable, and separately record required-unit coverage,
false-premise handling, highest asserted level, and limitation preservation. A third, distinct
agent invocation adjudicates disagreements before method identities are joined. Exact quantities,
identities, mask relations, and endpoint derivations remain code-scored. These labels are
agent-assessed, not human annotations; repeated calls to one model family may share systematic
errors. The retained Luna history below documents measurement development, but its earlier endpoint
qualification does not automatically validate the new five-way atomic-support task. That exact task
requires a bounded fresh held-out qualification before primary scoring.

Earlier v4--v6 judge configurations failed their prospectively fixed gates and remain ineligible.
The reference-audited v7 cycle retained the same Luna alias, high reasoning setting, prompt,
schema, isolation, two-pass rule, and thresholds over 24 fresh cases per pass. Both passes classified
20/20 composite endpoints and 32/32 required units correctly, with 176/187 and 179/187 core fields,
zero observed false rejections among 17 factual answers, zero observed false acceptances among six
unsupported answers, and no protected-test failures. That historical v7 result remains preserved.
Zero observed errors do not establish zero population error:
the upward-rounded Wilson 95\% upper bounds are 0.1844 for false rejection and 0.3904 for false
acceptance, and amendment~2's original bounds remain authoritative where applicable.

Before candidate-v3 activation, a prospectively frozen endpoint-threat extension applied the
unchanged judge to 24 new command--motion cases in each of two isolated passes. All 48 calls
completed, but composite accuracy was 16/20 and 17/20, core-field accuracy was 162/192 and
167/192, and both passes failed protected prompt-injection/boundary gates. Under the frozen hard
stop, the extension's smaller bounds are ineligible and Luna-scored semantic confirmation is
blocked. This later failure does not retroactively alter the historical v7 labels; it prevents
their use to activate the intended command--motion endpoint. No confirmatory response has been
scored and no alpha was consumed.

Subsequent v8--v11 cycles were retained as failures and exposed two evaluation defects: a client
warning was initially misclassified as tool use, and exact subtype scoring treated
``incorrect'' versus ``omitted'' as a primary coverage distinction while applying positive
mechanism labels to nominal or intentionally qualified answers. Before any confirmatory semantic
label, we froze a final fresh v12 qualification aligned to the declared endpoint. On diagnosable
cases, success requires coverage of the declared atomic mechanism unit and no material error;
nominal and ambiguous cases remain risk and qualification controls. Both isolated v12 passes
scored 8/8 endpoint and 28/28 unit-coverage decisions, with core-field accuracy 117/120 and
119/120, zero material false acceptance/rejection, zero protected failures, and zero call
failures. V12 is therefore eligible for future separately frozen packets under that historical
endpoint. It is automated
model-judge evidence, not human agreement; each eight-case material class has an approximately
0.3244 zero-error Wilson 95\% upper bound. A bounded complete-endpoint extension then classified
18/18 complete or incomplete answers and 72/72 essential-unit decisions correctly in each pass.
The evidence-complete campaign subsequently closed with 56 P/R pairs, 43 primary clusters after
two separately marked opaque-ID-only derivatives, and 13 controls. Five original judgments remain
invalid and immutable. The exact registered $N=24$ release is therefore invalid under its frozen
no-retry rule. The post-hoc corrected sensitivity gives a least-favourable difference of
$-0.2083$ with 98\% confidence sequence $[-0.5331,0.0800]$ and a most-favourable difference of
$0.25$ with $[-0.1838,0.6281]$. Neither establishes benefit; no replication exists.

We then froze a seven-packet development screen before any study judgment. It contains six
independent scenario configurations and one within-cluster evidence mask; only persistent
command--motion discrepancy and measured-response recovery enter the diagnostic-success endpoint.
Luna returned 26 of 28 valid judgments. Both invalid returns were R on the evidence mask and used
the wrong opaque response identifier; we retained them without retry. Among the 12 paired-valid
judgments per method, P had 10 material errors and covered 84/92 required units, whereas R had
8 material errors and covered 86/92. P made no causal overclaim and R made two, but P therefore
failed the prespecified material-risk and useful-coverage guardrails. Pass-specific endpoint
differences were 0.0 and 0.5 over only two eligible cases, and the passes disagreed on P for one
case. We reject this versioned candidate for confirmation and report no effect estimate.

A later coverage-complete candidate was tested against a stronger raw-evidence R on five fresh
development configurations and two within-cluster masks. Its first Luna arm is ineligible because
the judge packet omitted raw cells and samples from which R had derived several claims. Under a
separately frozen evidence-complete closure, all 28 judgments completed: P scored 5/5 eligible
clusters in each pass with 0/14 material errors and 104/104 required units; R scored 4/5 in each
pass with 2/14 material errors and 96/104 units. Crucially, R missed a different cluster in each
pass, leaving zero consensus independent P-win clusters. The frozen recurring-advantage and
two-family gates failed, so this candidate is also rejected and no confirmation was activated.

A subsequent bounded audit revisited the earlier measurement-complete v2 screen without changing
its responses or labels. Most assertions Luna marked unsupported in seven R answers---including
grid histograms and dimensions, event locators, action duration/error code, per-window medians,
and BT/configuration facts---were present in R's permitted method inputs or exact sources but absent
from the compact judge packet. Conversely, P's omitted trajectory magnitude was essential because
that historical question explicitly requested the measured trajectory change. We therefore do not
use v2's apparent 4/4 versus 1/4 scores as a fair pilot effect. We select unchanged deterministic
v2 only for a new atomic-question campaign whose evidence-complete packets expose the facts
available to both methods. The selection is prospective and consumes no alpha.

\section{Development Case: Terminal Margin}

We first exercised the complete path on a retained RoboBoat development run; it is not a held-out
result. Nav2 returned success at 0.3738~m goal error while independent odometry measured
0.04861~m/s. The exact configuration allowed 0.400~m XY error and 0.050~m/s stopped speed. Against
the declared 0.400~m task tolerance, only 0.0262~m margin remained. The vessel then moved 0.1876~m
and ended at 0.5614~m error. The checked answer therefore leads with an inadequate terminal margin,
connects the configured success to post-result motion, and withholds a unique claim about motor,
hydrodynamic, wind, current, wave, or collision causation.

A parity-audited development comparison gave R the same executable terminal-margin computation
and exact source/configuration as P. R, P/T, and even N expressed the central unmasked mechanism;
this one case provides no evidence that P improves on a strong tool-enabled agent. P's fluent
candidate failed the exact final-text check, so final P fell back to T.

A paired mask removed only measured speed at return. The original P/T plan then withheld the whole
mechanism, while R and N retained the still-supported positional-margin and post-return-motion
chain and withheld stopped-speed compliance. We retained this omission and corrected the plan only
for future use. The mask adds neither an independent episode nor a prospective result.

\section{Development Case: Geometric Restriction}

A current-source land run exercises the geometric path using only a delivered Nav2 global
costmap, delivered odometry, action timing, and exact configuration/source hashes. The requested
18~m centerline first intersects a cost-253 cell near $x=8.45$~m and has zero minimum clearance
from a lethal cell. The retained configuration uses a 0.22~m robot radius and 0.55~m inflation.
The robot moved as far as $x=6.949$~m, deviated 2.705~m laterally, and 69 planning updates returned
success before the action aborted in 70.860~s under an exact 70~s behavior-tree deadline.

Crucially, a separately versioned deterministic costmap audit finds that the retained grid still connects the
action-result pose to the goal below cost 253. The checked answer therefore reports a direct-route
restriction, substantial detour, and deadline-aligned abort; it does not claim global no-path,
name the evaluator-only obstacle, or attribute the result to recovery exhaustion. The terminal
behavior-tree transition was not delivered, so source semantics plus timing reconstruct the
deadline mechanism but do not directly observe its final tick. This development case is retained
as partial fault induction rather than tuned or discarded. It tests response usefulness and
causal restraint, not comparative effectiveness.

The first information-parity development comparison produced a useful negative result. Once R
received the same executable geometric audit as P, R communicated the same route restriction,
connected-grid qualification, detour, deadline alignment, and limits as P/T; this single case gives
no evidence that P outperforms a tool-enabled repository agent. P's fluent candidate was
substantively faithful but failed the conservative exact-text check, so its final answer fell back
to T. In these initial cases, final P was therefore a deterministic method, not evidence that
language realization improved the response.

Three further geometric checks delimit this result. Removing costmap cells made every method
withhold the physical restriction, but P/T initially also omitted the still-supported deadline
alignment. An independently recomputed S-turn case reproduced a direct-route intersection,
retained-grid connectivity, 1.699~m lateral deviation, and abort 0.918~s from the same deadline; R
again matched P/T on project review. A predeclared nominal control succeeded with complete route
coverage, no cost-253 intersection, and only 0.084~m maximum lateral deviation. These masks and
controls exposed false-premise and evidence-coverage bugs, which were retained and corrected only
for future use.

Two additional records exercise execution provenance without claiming physical causation. A
warehouse success establishes at least four source-qualified recovery invocations after recorded
planner-branch failures, but not their physical cause or whole-history completeness. A no-retry
instrumentation run retained 12,975 poses across 70 unique delivered plans; an independent
implementation reproduced all hashes and deviations, including a direct first plan and later
$-1.175$ to $+1.035$~m deviations. In both, tool-enabled R matched the central diagnosis on project
review, and P fell back to T. Separately, a later scenario-binding-qualified route-change record
was scored as a bounded development check under the then-qualified Luna v7 arm: both isolated passes marked P,
R, and T successful and N unsuccessful, yielding P-minus-R 0.0 in each pass. P and T were
byte-identical, yet one pass assigned them different secondary full/partial dispositions; we
retain that judge instability and do not seek a third vote. The warehouse and earlier
instrumentation records remain unscored development observations.

\section{Development Case: Command--Motion Discrepancy}

We next qualified synchronized command and independently delivered odometry evidence on a
predeclared held-condition/nominal development pair. The blind held-condition export contains 376
command samples and 1,598 goal-interval odometry samples. A fixed-window computation, reproduced
by a separate evaluator implementation, calibrates a healthy median measured response of
0.2597~m/s and identifies the earliest sustained discrepancy from 7--17~s: median delivered
command remains 0.800~m/s while measured planar motion is 0.000~m/s. The retained execution then

This evidence supports an execution-level command--motion discrepancy connected to failure of
continued progress. It does not prove downstream actuator acceptance, Nav2 consumption of the

matched nominal run uses the same thresholds, succeeds after 9.480~m, records neither
\texttt{FollowPath} failure nor source-qualified \texttt{Wait}, and correctly returns
\texttt{not\_triggered}. Thus the checked answer rejects rather than merely qualifies the false

A separately declared compensation case produced the same independently reproduced 8--18~s

again fell back under the operating verifier. This is a plausible differentiation case, not a
qualified label. Conversely, removing all independent odometry caused every method to withhold the
discrepancy; the execution sequence remained answerable. These cases test outcome independence and
appropriate abstention without adding independent evidence through masks.

\section{Development Findings and Disposition}

For the redirected evidence-calibration pilot, the deterministic full-system arm completed its
fixed development ladders and passed claim-aware verification. The strong-agent canaries produced
no valid response because of retained initialization and transport failures, after which admission
stopped. No failed request was retried, no human annotation was collected, and no comparative
estimate is available. This partial execution validates neither an advantage nor a disadvantage
for either method and does not unlock the prospective semantic freeze.

The artifact proves that the retained measurements can be recomputed and rendered into bounded
diagnoses. It does not prove that P improves supported diagnostic success over R. Project review
found frequent parity once R received the same executable tool, one promising compensation error
by R, a qualified two-pass route-change tie, and repeated conservative-verifier failures by P.
The first frozen checked-composition screen supplied blinded development labels but still did not
establish an advantage: an unsupported certificate/registry footer made P materially erroneous in
most paired-valid judgments, and omissions reduced its required-unit coverage below R. R's two
unsupported recovery-causation claims show the intended causal-restraint benefit, but the complete
prespecified gate rejects P rather than selecting that favorable component alone.
A measurement-complete successor appeared to show a repeated endpoint advantage and lower error
risk, but lost required-unit coverage and was rejected at its exact no-degradation guardrail. The
later bounded audit found its compact judge packet omitted facts available to R, while some P
omissions were essential to the questions asked; those apparent scores cannot support a fair
effect. A further
coverage-complete successor repaired risk and coverage, yet the strong raw-evidence R missed
different clusters across the two Luna passes. With zero consensus P-win clusters, it failed the
predeclared recurrence and mechanism-family gates. Thus the sequence supports the feasibility of
faithful deterministic composition, not a stable semantic advantage over R. A predecessor redirect
then selected complete supported communication of an atomic mechanism, decisive measurement,
outcome, and causal limit. Its closed campaign's corrected sensitivity is highly dependent on
repeated-judge disagreement and establishes no positive effect. Its labels also show the motivating
tradeoff: P omitted required content while R accumulated many Luna-assessed substantive-error
labels. Every inspected case remains development evidence. The new study does not revive either
of those P-contract superiority claims; it uses the failures to motivate controlled measurement of
specificity as robot-visible evidence is added or removed. The subsequent v4 control pilot did not
change that conclusion.
Two qualified missing-odometry cases produced unanimous P wins on complete communication, but the
separately frozen missing-command judge extension failed, leaving two additional response pairs
unscored. We do not select the favorable control slice as a headline population. A final fresh
causal-language judge qualification also failed its protected gates: one pass falsely rejected
supported chronology and both passes disagreed with the frozen field treatment of a response that
stated a causal limit and then contradicted it. Consequently no candidate-revision alpha was bound
and confirmation remains at $N=0$.
The subsequent fixed-suffix P-contract-v4 pilot likewise did not justify confirmation. Thirteen
primary clusters completed both blinded Luna passes after two of 15 planned primary captures
failed frozen physical gates without replacement. Under the required least-favourable mapping,
both complete M/Q/O/L communication and substantive assertion reliability were exact P--R ties.
Only one Claim-A P advantage appeared under the most-favourable treatment of judge disagreement
(difference $1/13=0.0769$), below the frozen two-net-discordance promotion rule. P was shorter
(median 95 versus 172 words) and used no model call versus 17 R calls, but these secondary
differences do not establish either semantic claim. A control-only signal is not promoted: three
R limitation fields disagreed between passes, and an independent raw-sample computation showed
that one consensus ``unsupported measurement'' label was itself incorrect.
We also tested a narrower, prospectively frozen causal-restraint successor. A finite detector
initially marked six of fourteen R answers and none of the P answers, but its required exact-span
audit found that all seven matches were embedded in explicit non-establishment statements. The
baseline had followed the strengthened causal instruction; the detector failed to represent the
surrounding scope. Confirmed violations were therefore zero for both methods, and this successor
was closed without Luna scoring, confirmation, or alpha use.
The first fresh development canary was unfavorable to P-contract v1: both isolated Luna passes
marked its decisive-comparison field incomplete because the deterministic text omitted the
recovered commanded median, while R-contract was complete in both passes. One pass also marked
P's outcome scope incomplete. Neither answer had a substantive material error. We retain that
result and treat the ensuing v2 rendering repair as a new development version requiring fresh
evidence; it does not increase confirmatory $N$.
Two single-attempt composition captures were also retained rather than tuned: the first failed
scenario construction, while the second authenticated its scenario but did not reach a terminal

of the latter supported a bounded command--motion discrepancy while withholding geometry and the

extension failed and blocked candidate-v3 semantic confirmation. A fresh endpoint-first v8 cycle
then classified all 48 composite cases correctly in each pass with zero material-error polarity

Subsequent failed cycles were retained, and the fresh endpoint-aligned v12 arm eventually qualified
for future separately frozen packets. Qualification repaired the scoring bottleneck but did not
select a treatment or create an effect. These inspected development
outputs cannot become a held-out method comparison. The focused campaign is now closed; its
original release is invalid and its corrected result is explicitly post-hoc sensitivity evidence.
Reusing those inspected labels as fresh P-contract confirmation, or turning their risk--coverage
pattern into a significance claim, would overstate the evidence.
The final contract-complete promotion route is therefore closed for this submission snapshot. The
artifact retains the negative and inconclusive evidence, the two narrow missing-motion control
wins, and the failed judge qualifications; it does not report a statistically significant method
advantage or a replication result.

motivation for claim-level calibration, not a significance claim: the inventories are finite, the
endpoints followed answer inspection, and absence of a match does not certify semantic correctness.

\section{Limitations and Validity}

The diagnostic families are deliberately narrow. A correct result applies only to its retained
model, signals, interval, and assumptions. Delivered observations do not prove controller
consumption; local geometry does not prove global infeasibility; a command--motion residual does
not uniquely identify a physical source. Evaluator truth is unavailable to ordinary methods.
Consequently, agreement with physical truth cannot excuse a claim unsupported by the corresponding
robot-visible evidence condition. Conversely, conservative wording is not automatically good: an
answer that withholds an available supported diagnosis incurs an under-specificity or
over-abstention error.

Experiments use simulation. They can test controlled mechanisms and simulation-based ecological
relevance, not hardware reliability or real-world transfer. The study measures factual and
diagnostic outcomes, not human trust. The original Luna v7 arm passed both fresh held-out
qualification passes, but its later targeted endpoint-threat extension failed both passes. The
subsequent v8--v11 cycles likewise failed their complete prospective gates. V12 qualified the
endpoint-aligned automated arm, but the later campaign's five transport-invalid judgments
prevented its original registered release. The post-hoc correction does not change the small
held-out categories or wide systematic-error bounds. Repeated-model measurements do not
constitute independent human agreement, and the new atomic-support annotation task remains
unqualified. The failed composition audit further shows that judge qualification cannot repair
an incomplete case reference. Its small error-category denominators also yield large finite-sample sensitivity
bounds that a future nominal advantage may fail. The legacy cohort stopped below its
frozen minimum and has no completed semantic scoring, so it supports neither an effect estimate nor
a significance claim. The development cases were selected to exercise the pipeline; only a
separately frozen prospective study can support comparative claims.

Finally, deterministic rendering may outperform or dominate language-model realization. At this
development operating point it supplied every final P answer; without prospective study labels we
report that fallback frequency but do not infer comparative quality or attribute templated final
answers to language-model reasoning.

\section{Conclusion}

Trustworthy robot explanations should not merely be correct when sufficient information happens to
be available. Their causal and mechanistic specificity should track what the robot can justify.
We formalize that requirement with atomic claims, evidence contracts, diagnostic abstraction
levels, and non-entailments; operationalize it through nested evidence interventions on fixed robot
episodes; and evaluate a diagnostic-contract method through unsupported-specificity risk and
useful coverage. Development evidence shows why this reframing is necessary: fair tool-enabled
agents often recover the same central diagnosis, deterministic fallback dominated the original
pipeline, and several candidate or judge campaigns were negative, inconclusive, or invalid for
confirmation. We preserve those outcomes and make no superiority claim. The prospective study
therefore tests evidence calibration rather than which system appears to reason better, with
episodes---not masks, questions, generations, or annotations---as its independent units.
