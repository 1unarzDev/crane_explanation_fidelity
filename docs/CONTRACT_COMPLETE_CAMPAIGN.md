## 4. Field-level automated evaluation

Luna remains an automated evidence auditor under the currently qualified model/settings,
isolation, two-pass, no-quality-retry, and tolerance policies. Each answer is a separate blinded
call. Neither pass sees the other pass, method identity, verifier/fallback status, or other answers.

The annotation packet binds four required units named `M`, `Q`, `O`, and `L`. Luna returns each
unit's status, exact response span, justification, claim-level support records, evidence
references, and material-error fields. Quantities, intervals, thresholds, hashes, and identities
are checked deterministically when possible. Absence from a compact reference inventory is not
itself evidence that an additional true statement is unsupported.

`analysis/score_contract_complete_judgment.py` computes the endpoints:

```text
Claim A success = M covered AND Q covered AND O covered AND L covered
                  AND no substantive material error

Claim B event   = at least one substantive unsupported or contradicted assertion
```

Omission fails Claim A without automatically becoming a Claim B error. A definite missing or
incorrect component fails Claim A; a definite substantive error fails both appropriate endpoints;
otherwise unresolved is retained. Cosmetic identifier formatting can be excluded only after a
separate deterministic check establishes that it is the sole issue. Meaning-changing source
attribution and runtime/source linkage remain substantive. Broader original error categories are
retained as secondary outcomes.

Two-pass disagreement is never resolved by another Luna vote. Independently checkable facts or a
frozen rule may resolve a field; otherwise least-/most-favourable analyses retain the uncertainty
and identify the responsible M/Q/O/L field.

The first bounded extension (`contract-complete-luna-qualification-v1`) was executed and failed.
Its generic missing-evidence unit text conflicted with the intended withholding/count contract, its
expected hypothesis label contradicted the rubric, and secondary category-name mismatches were
incorrectly blocking. All 24 calls and the failed report are retained. A single corrected v2 is
frozen with entirely fresh cases, question-specific units, corrected unsupported-hypothesis
semantics, and only endpoint-changing fields as blocking gates; the judge prompt/settings are
unchanged. V2 then qualified in both isolated passes: 12/12 endpoint decisions per pass, 47/48
exact unit decisions per pass, zero material false acceptances/rejections, and zero protected-causal
failures. Secondary answerability, abstention, and category-name mismatches remain reportable. V2
targets:

- omission versus fabrication;
- recovery versus eventual task outcome;
- unsupported causal connectives;
- numeric value/interval scope;
- valid additional facts absent from a compact required-unit inventory;
- instruction-like evidence text.

Minor secondary-field mismatches are reported and do not automatically veto the campaign.
Endpoint-changing error or method-specific judge bias does.

## 5. Prospective claims and reporting hierarchy

Both claims must be registered together before confirmation.

**Claim A — complete supported diagnostic communication.** P-contract increases the probability
of M/Q/O/L communication without substantive material error relative to R-contract.

**Claim B — substantive assertion reliability.** P-contract reduces response-level substantive
unsupported/contradicted assertion risk relative to R-contract.

For each claim report:

- Level A: corrected inference establishes a positive method difference;
- Level B: the lower bound also exceeds a prospectively fixed +0.10 absolute improvement;
- Level C: secondary coverage, controls, breadth, latency, cost, and other tradeoffs support a
  broader preference.

Claim A uses P-minus-R success. Claim B uses R-minus-P substantive-error risk so positive values
favor P. Claim selection after outcomes is prohibited. Either Level A result may be the focused
paper contribution after multiplicity correction; it licenses only its own claim.
