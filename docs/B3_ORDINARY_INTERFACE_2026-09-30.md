# B3 ordinary-realization interface — 2026-09-30

The protocol specifies a deterministic diagnostic contract/planner followed by ordinary language
realization for B3, without final claim verification. The new development helpers prepare that
ordinary request and retain its answer. They reuse the existing diagnostic engine, realization-plan
validator and five-method packet surface. They have no provider caller, retry loop, workspace
stager or pilot launch command. Exact model configuration, permitted workspace, tool budgets and
durable execution declaration remain open.

## Request boundary

The enclosing runner must supply governed robot-visible requirement facts. The engine checks their
condition/packet binding and derives the supported plan. This helper does not independently qualify
the computations or infer episode truth. The realization plan must match the derived result and
retain approved numeric slots and required limitations. The paired method packets must retain their
hashes, exact visible evidence, question and episode/condition identity. B2/B3/B4 share evidence,
source assets and primitive tools; B3/B4 share contract assets. B3's verification flag must be false.
Evaluator-only keys are rejected from supplied evidence, facts, method packets and exported context.

The model context contains the recorded question, full permitted evidence, public source/tool
identities and the selected approved propositions, required/optional status, quantities, units,
support references, limitations, missing requirements and ambiguity. It does not expose the sibling
method packets or an evaluator answer. V2 non-entailment wording supplies explicit cause limits.
The prompt asks for the required content, useful partial findings, causal restraint, citations and
false-premise rejection as ordinary prose. It is a bound development template, not an execution
freeze or a demonstrated model configuration.

## Return boundary

After a separately authorized one-shot invocation, the helper checks request identity, transport
success, prompt/schema identity and raw/parsed consistency of the ordinary answer object. It
preserves the answer exactly, including whitespace, omissions, incorrect quantities or unsupported
assertions. Even an empty string is valid under the retained answer schema and is not a technical
reason for replacement. No claim checking, local removal, reconstruction or fallback runs on B3's
answer. Semantic assessment and endpoint treatment remain separate governed measurement steps.
Technical failures remain retained by the enclosing durable caller and cannot trigger quality-driven
retry. This helper provides no execution authorization or missing-response policy.

## Interpretation and tests

The active protocol's ordinary B3 path differs from B4's claim-ID-aware constrained realization as
well as its verification/repair. Consequently this candidate assesses their combined realization
and verification contribution. It cannot support a pure final-verifier-only causal claim. A later
shared-realization experiment would need a separate prospective scope decision; no such experiment
is substituted here. The protocol's older “isolates the final verification layer” wording remains
preserved, with a dated prospective clarification in the newest decision and this note.

Eleven synthetic checks verify supported-plan export, v2 limitation wording, unchanged inputs,
verbatim retention of five ordinary-answer forms, parity/binding/leakage rejection, raw/parsed
consistency and technical-failure handling. These are offline interface checks, not semantic
annotation, model behavior, independent experimental samples or evidence of a comparative effect.
No actual pilot request or response is generated. Run
`python -m pytest -q tests/test_evidence_calibration_b3_ordinary.py` and
`python analysis/prepare_evidence_calibration_b3_ordinary.py` for reproduction and binding integrity.

The bound interface candidate authorizes no model/annotation call or P11 freeze. The failed combined
