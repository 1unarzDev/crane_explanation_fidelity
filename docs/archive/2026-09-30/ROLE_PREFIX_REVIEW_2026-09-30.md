# Development review of the retained role prefix — 2026-09-30

## Scope

This project review covers all 69 original A-pass answers and their 669 returned atomic
stance/kind/polarity tuples. It uses the method-blind answer/inventory bundle and original
returns. No method key, visible-evidence packet, evaluator support label, or physical truth was
opened for this review. This is agent-assisted development review, not support annotation,
independent human validation, or endpoint qualification. The accompanying manifest binds the
reviewed records and representative findings. The separately running continuation is outside
this snapshot.

## Findings and dispositions

1. **Exact-input kind variance.** Four complete-answer/claim/span groups differ in kind while
   agreeing in stance and polarity. Two concern navigation abort (`TASK_OUTCOME` versus
   `SOFTWARE_ACTION_EVENT`); two concern command-stream validity (`COMMAND_OBSERVATION` versus
   `SOURCE_OR_CONFIG_FACT`). Preserve every return. A blanket kind-to-rank conversion could
   manufacture different scores for equivalent assertions.

2. **Record attribution needs claim-specific attachment.** The qualified codebook assigns
   record-only trace contents to `SOURCE_OR_CONFIG_FACT`, but reviewed trace-attributed failures,
   recoveries, and attempts received event kinds. These are application review concerns, not
   corrected labels. Source facts cannot all be exempted from evidential requirements: the
   existing `claim-recovery-invoked` contract explicitly covers a retained source-qualified trace
   recording invocation, with `req-recovery-trace-valid` and `req-recovery-invoked`. Configured
   policy and episode trace assertions require different evidence despite sharing a broad kind.

3. **Diagnostic non-trigger is underdefined in the generic kind codebook.** “Registered failure
   diagnostics did not trigger” received `SOFTWARE_ACTION_EVENT`/`NEGATIVE`; explicit
   command-motion non-trigger language elsewhere received `COMMAND_MOTION_RELATION`/`NEGATIVE`.
   Resolve which registered computation a claim invokes through its meaning and context before
   attaching requirements. A software-event tag alone does not settle false-premise correctness.

4. **Literal deictic limitations stay unresolved.** Old references to “these specific physical
   causes” cannot acquire a supplied antecedent from a reviewer or template. Preserve literal
   text and allow the qualified support task to use `UNINTERPRETABLE` where appropriate. The
   unresolved-label endpoint disposition remains prospective.

5. **Explicit negative causation remains an assertion.** Three reviewed atoms explicitly say
   measured response recovery “does not cause” the eventual outcome. Their returned
   `ASSERTED_FACT`/`RECOVERY_CAUSAL_RELATION`/`NEGATIVE` tuples preserve that unfavorable assertion.
   Do not convert them to statements that causation is merely unestablished. The separate v2
   wording repair applies only to future outputs.

6. **Numerical interval tags do not define diagnostic depth.** Interval atoms vary between
   `OTHER` and `SOURCE_OR_CONFIG_FACT`, and between `POSITIVE` and `NOT_APPLICABLE`. Quantities,
   units, interval bounds, and exact spans remain mechanically checkable. These kind/polarity
   differences do not supply an endpoint rank.

## Remaining measurement gates

The synthetic qualification remains valid within its frozen stance/kind/polarity scope. This
review does not regrade it or qualify support, mechanism flags, or asserted abstraction levels.
Both-pass application review and the missing-A disposition remain open. Any claim attachment
must handle asserted, hedged, negative, unendorsed, limiting, and unresolved propositions using
the existing contracts and non-entailments, with explicit treatment of unmatched claims.

The current support-packet builder requires `asserted_abstraction_level` for each atom. The
qualified role task does not provide it, and extractor levels cannot anchor scoring. Therefore
support handoff still needs a validated attachment/abstraction treatment or a separately
qualified prospective payload amendment. Do not substitute unqualified extractor ranks or use
the support annotator's raw highest-level field directly as endpoint rank.

No labels were corrected and no support labels, endpoint scores, effects, or p-values were
generated by this review. P11 remains closed; independent confirmation and replication N are
zero. A fresh five-method development pilot remains necessary before freezing comparisons,
coverage, power, or sample allocation.
