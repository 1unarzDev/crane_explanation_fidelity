# Diagnostic-study state and next testable claim

**Audit date:** 2026-09-25
**Scope:** living decision support for the new physical-diagnosis study. Frozen F/G/H artifacts,
historical model outputs, and prior failed qualification cycles remain unchanged.

## Decision in one sentence

The highest-value next candidate is **mandatory finite checked composition followed by
deterministic evidence-locked rendering**, compared with a fair tool-enabled repository agent that
receives the same robot-visible evidence, source/configuration, primitive diagnostic calculations,
model strength, and resource budget but not P's episode-specific certificate or checked answer
plan. Do not activate confirmation until this treatment repeatedly beats R on independent
development configurations from more than one mechanism family without worse material error,
useful coverage, or ambiguity handling.

This is a candidate selection decision, not an effectiveness result. A tie or R advantage remains
a valid outcome.

## Why prior candidates usually tie R

The strongest R already receives repository source, configuration, robot-visible evidence, and
the same executable primitive diagnostic. On a single-mechanism packet, a capable agent can often
repeat the diagnostic result and its limits. P therefore has little remaining treatment contrast;
prompt polish, more fluent realization, or another isolated mechanism is unlikely to create a
scientifically meaningful advantage.

The recurring failures that remain addressable are composition failures:

- omitting the decisive interval, comparator, count, unit, or outcome relation;
- promoting a logged obstacle assertion or temporal ordering into physical causation;
- treating delivered observations as proof of Nav2 consumption;
- carrying a diagnosis across a mask that removed its decisive evidence;
- withholding useful supported execution facts when a deeper cause is unresolved; and
- dropping a supported mechanism during language realization or conservative verification.

A mandatory checked certificate targets all six. It is not presented as a novel logic formalism;
the prospective contribution is the tested diagnosis-to-language policy and its measured effect
on CRANE episodes.

## Candidate treatment and fair baseline

### P: checked composition and evidence-locked rendering

1. Run the same independently tested primitive diagnostics available to R.
2. Translate only validated outputs into versioned predicates.
3. Enumerate every state admitted by the finite registry and retained observations.
4. Select only an entailed mechanism, otherwise return an explicit unresolved/evidence-problem
   state with its missing discriminator.
5. Compile the mechanism's required measurements, execution link, outcome, and limits into a
   checked plan.
6. Render deterministically. Fail closed rather than emit language if any required unit is absent.

The model-relative scope, registry hash, support identifiers, unresolved alternatives, and
`out_of_model_possible=true` must remain visible in the certificate. The treatment must not receive
evaluator-only intervention labels.

### R: strongest fair repository-aware alternative

R receives the identical robot-visible packet, exact relevant source/configuration, retrieval,
primitive diagnostic tools, deterministic calculation tools, and comparable or greater model,
token, call, and wall-time budget. It may inspect the generic composer source and rules or use an
equivalent generic constraint tool. It does not receive P's episode-specific certificate, selected
mechanism, or checked answer plan. All tool calls and returned values are retained.

This comparison tests a mandatory verified orchestration policy, not privileged evidence or a
weakened language model. If R independently reconstructs the certificate, a tie is the correct
result.

## Development gate before confirmation

First run deterministic conformance and mutation tests; these do not count as robot episodes or
effect evidence. Then use a fixed development screen of independent scenario configurations. A
question variant, evidence mask, repeated generation, candidate revision, or Luna pass remains
inside its originating cluster.

The screen must include at least the following distinct configuration families:

1. persistent command--motion discrepancy;
2. measured response recovery/successful compensation;
3. geometric restriction and an execution failure with a similar terminal symptom;
4. nominal success with a false failure premise;
5. missing decisive evidence;
6. irrelevant or robot-visible-but-not-proven-consumed obstacle evidence; and
7. ambiguous or out-of-model behavior.

Cases 5--7 are guardrails; they are not relabeled as positive-mechanism cases. A mask paired with a
complete packet tests qualification but adds zero independent configurations. Development may use
already inspected records to reject a candidate cheaply, but only fresh, predeclared configurations
can enter confirmation or replication.

Do not freeze P unless both isolated v12 passes show all of the following on the complete fixed
screen:

- P has more supported-diagnostic successes than R in at least two mechanism families;
- P has no material-error excess;
- P's useful required-unit coverage is not lower;
- every missing-evidence, visible-not-consumed, and ambiguous case is appropriately qualified;
- independently checked measurements and references are complete before judging; and
- the observed separation is large enough to make the immutable +0.15 worthwhile-improvement
  target plausible under the registered judge-sensitivity analysis.

Failure closes that candidate version. Do not tune against the same outputs or weaken R.

## Prospective campaign if—and only if—the development gate passes

Freeze a new campaign namespace before collecting its first outcome. The target population must be
balanced across the seven families above and must include matched terminal symptoms from geometry
and execution mechanisms. Use one primary diagnostic question per independent configuration;
secondary questions, masks, and Luna passes remain clustered with it. Keep fault-induction success
separate from recording validity and retain valid unexpected outcomes.

The existing sequential protocol's +0.15 minimum worthwhile improvement, material-error/useful-
coverage/ambiguity guardrails, cumulative alpha ledger, two-pass v12 handling, and disjoint fresh
replication reserve remain the starting constraints. Recalculate required sample size and group or
anytime-valid boundaries from the selected candidate's multi-family development rates; do not
borrow the old 40-episode or single-family estimates. Statistical significance is a possible
result of the frozen design, not a completion criterion that permits sampling or metric changes.

The primary endpoint remains Luna-assessed supported diagnostic success on independently labeled
diagnosable questions: the required supported physical/execution mechanism is covered and the
answer contains no material unsupported or contradicted assertion. Report deterministic reference
measurements separately, along with material-error risk, useful coverage, ambiguity qualification,
judge disagreement/unresolved sensitivity, resources, and deterministic-rendering frequency.
