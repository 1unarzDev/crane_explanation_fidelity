Invalid/tool/error returns are retained as technical failures. Word count and adherence to the unchanged 60-word instruction are recorded without silently truncating, repairing or retrying the baseline. The deterministic contract remains constrained to three clauses and 60 words. Runtime model snapshot, full decoding/system/environment binding and semantic evaluation remain unresolved before freeze.

The immutable declaration and terminal report live in `development_rich_methods_v1/`. Do not infer terminal results from partial claims or preliminary valid returns. No scoring or hypothesis test is executed by this runner, and answer count does not increase independent N. All six underlying episodes remain permanently excluded from confirmation regardless of outputs. The earlier limited 18-packet method pilot and all its technical failures remain unchanged.
