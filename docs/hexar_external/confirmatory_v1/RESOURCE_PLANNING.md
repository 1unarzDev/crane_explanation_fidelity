# Outcome-blind acquisition resource forecast

The completed 144-attempt reliability campaign supplies original capture observations: mean 53.52 seconds, maximum 62.57 seconds; mean original raw data 5.92 MB, maximum 7.09 MB. Every measured receipt and captured file was checked against its original bytes. No explanation, judge label or superiority result enters this forecast.

HX-CONTRACT has six deterministic realizations per episode and no method-model call. Each method has six unique outputs per episode; two independent initial judgments per output give 24N initial judgments, with at most 12N disagreement-only C judgments. Thus maximum method-plus-judge calls are 42N; without C, 30N. Calls and battery cells do not increase independent N.

The existing conditional N=3,072/.20-invalidity example permits at most 4,074 attempts (167 reserves per family). At the observed capture mean this is about 60.6 capture hours. This is not an adopted N/reserve decision, a time guarantee, or permission to continue after a finite cap. Native review, admission/inventory, filesystem, sealing and provider durations add to these capture-only quantities. Derived evidence/transport/semantic archives add to raw-file storage. No monetary estimate is made without an actual provider cost basis.

These observations inform feasibility alongside the existing power sensitivity. They do not prove future technical independence, a population invalidity cap, semantic effect or completion probability, and do not select N. The reproducible code/report are `resource_planning_v1.py` and `manifests/hexar_external/acquisition/resource_planning_v1.json`. No alpha or scientific protocol changed.
