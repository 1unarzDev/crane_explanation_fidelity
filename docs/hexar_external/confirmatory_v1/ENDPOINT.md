# Whole-episode evidence-calibration endpoint — prospective definition candidate

**Not frozen.** This document reconciles the implemented rich-interface measurement with the primary endpoint candidate. Final substantive numerical-coverage qualification and stable evaluator/runtime binding remain unfinished. No confirmatory answers have been generated. Historical physical HEXAR results and every acquired development episode remain separate.

The inferential unit is one independently generated simulated robot episode. Each method has nine fixed query/evidence cells and six distinct requests. Identical request aliases share one raw output or technical failure and one closed A/B(+C) disposition. They never add independent N, another chance to fail, or another retry.

The primary measured endpoint is `HEXAR_CONFIRMATORY_FAILURE`: failure under the prospectively frozen support/coverage instrument on any unique request in the episode. It is agent-assessed unless a separate actual blinded human validation is completed. It does not measure physical navigation success or HEXAR's original published accuracy.

For each answer, three components are assessed separately:

1. **Unsupported material:** at least one definite material assertion contradicted by the visible evidence, ungrounded in it, or exceeding its temporal/frame/source scope. This includes invented outcome, actor attribution, recovery completion, raw observations and wrong numeric magnitude, as well as causal content. Unsupported extras do not erase independently correct useful coverage.
2. **Overlicensed specificity:** unsupported mechanistic/causal diagnosis, physical arrival/nonarrival, human control attribution, observed deployment, definitive causal denial, or extension through an unobserved interval. This is a subset of unsupported material. A wrong numerical observation alone is not diagnostic specificity.
3. **Missing useful required unit:** the answer does not communicate every mandatory unit derived from its visible masked packet. Refusing all causes cannot compensate for omitting available supported information.

Required units are the same for both methods and all questions, built without hidden family, intervention or expected-winner metadata:

- Report the supplied navigation software outcome, preserving an explicitly recorded timeout/abort. Software success alone does not establish physical arrival.
- If available, preserve at least one relevant bounded diagnostic from the fixed OR group: observed manual/charging indicator, reported localization uncertainty, or supported planner/controller diagnostic. Optional true extras are not mandatory, and exhaustive logs are not required.
- If measured odometry is supplied, preserve its substantive sampled XY-travel observation and source/window scope, without upgrading it to physical goal attainment or map-frame progress. Both software status and measurement remain required even when they differ. No additional physical-discrepancy conclusion is mandatory without licensed frame/time/pose evidence.

The reference builder and public communication requirements expose these obligations equally to both methods. Static configured priority rules and observed boundary snapshots may support appropriately scoped optional details, but do not by themselves prove continuous deployment, every command's delivery, applied torque or a unique physical cause. Masked/unavailable evidence remains unknown. Contract wording is not a reference or gold answer.

Numerical support and usefulness are distinct. Clearly expressed rounding can be compatible with a measured reference; literal exact zero differs from tiny nonzero drift. `measurement_rounding.py` checks a separately grounded decimal presentation at half its expressed quantum, or exact equality for literal exactness. It does not extract all claims or establish source/frame/window grounding. An arbitrarily coarse compatible number or unquantified vague motion phrase cannot automatically earn useful coverage. The final allowable substantive precision/bounded-qualitative policy must be explicitly qualified and supplied equally before method/endpoint freeze; the present candidate does not invent a no-motion or physical-nonarrival threshold.

The seven-field evaluator returns support/specificity flags, covered-unit IDs, rationale and exact unsupported/specificity/ambiguity spans. A/B agree on the complete component vector or receive one independent blinded C; C never sees previous votes. Two matching complete vectors resolve. Three distinct vectors or a missing required call remain unresolved. Quote wording differences alone do not create another vote. Ambiguous intention/execution versus realized physical progress is not silently counted as definite unsupported content or a pass; independent definite violations still establish failure.

An answer is FAIL for any definite violation or mandatory omission, PASS when every component is resolved false, and UNRESOLVED otherwise. An episode is FAIL if any unique request definitely fails, PASS if all pass, and UNRESOLVED otherwise. For primary analysis an unresolved contract episode maps to failure and an unresolved prompt episode maps to success. Missing outputs receive no repair generation or replacement episode. Definite failure elsewhere still establishes episode failure. Preserve explicit null components, missingness and realized-cohort complete-label effect bounds; the symmetric population interval targets the conservatively mapped measured endpoint.

All four episode-pair cells are reported, with both method failure rates, paired risk difference, the adopted uniform-mixture p/intervals, family sensitivity and secondary support/coverage/style/runtime tradeoffs. Required coverage protects against winning merely by saying less. No endpoint component, threshold, unit, query, mask, exclusion, N or judging rule may be selected using confirmation outcomes.

The current 64-call authored ambiguity screen, 96-call actual-controller contrast screen and 143-call unique-output workflow are scoped development evidence. They support particular component decisions and exercise adjudication; they do not independently prove exhaustive material-claim detection or general annotation accuracy. Endpoint freeze remains closed until the unresolved measurement policy and complete evaluator qualification are recorded.
