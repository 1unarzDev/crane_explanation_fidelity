# Durable raw-cohort acquisition schedule candidate

`raw_schedule_v1.py` adds a separate candidate confirmation schedule generator without modifying the development planner. An independently drawn 256-bit master seed deterministically generates collision-checked episode IDs and 32-bit simulator seeds. Six families are interleaved in a fixed order; each has a valid quota and finite reserve. The plan is explicitly **CANDIDATE_NOT_ADMITTED**, with no confirmation authorization. No real confirmation identities have been allocated or acquired by this engineering work.

The controller reserves one durable claim before each launch. It stops a family at its frozen valid quota, uses only the next allocated within-family attempt following technical invalidity, and terminates without a balanced cohort if any family exhausts its cap. It never increases N or reserves from outcomes. These are outcome-blind technical stopping rules, not semantic optional stopping. All failures remain in the journal.

A persistent execution root binds the complete schedule and acquisition/development hash. Every operation rechecks independent admission and saved bindings. The schedule controller enforces global interleaving and within-family prefixes. A process lock prevents a second caller from disposing a live attempt as crashed. A durable claim after interruption is closed conservatively as indeterminate, preserving captured artifacts and never rerunning the episode. This candidate does not recover an interrupted deterministic review; such a recovery policy would need separate prospective qualification.

Capture and technical review remain separate, independently qualified adapters. Their archives are hash-bound in the closed disposition. Raw capture must report no method or judge generation, and review must report no method-outcome access. Reported/unknown semantic exposure creates a permanent contamination marker and stops the acquisition controller; it cannot be repaired by a reserve replacement. Changed archives, schedule, bindings or unallocated attempts fail before another launch.

Fourteen synthetic tests cover deterministic fresh allocation, balanced interleaving, finite reserves, retained failures, crash/no-reissue behavior, concurrent calls, denied/revoked admission, archive changes, skipped identities, root rebinding and permanent contamination closure. These tests establish scheduling behavior only; no simulator, provider, endpoint score or project significance test is called.

**This is not the final acquisition adapter.** Production admission must independently verify the committed prospective family/scientific/acquisition freeze, actual fixed N/reserves and seed schedule, current freshness exclusions, qualified runtime/source/image and exact root. The controller's injectable admission callback is an engineering seam, not confirmation permission. Production capture must qualify the same physics/reset/family rules with phase/binding provenance written before launch. The final reviewer must reproduce native raw/source/evidence checks and export every valid/invalid disposition for sealing, including attempts where no bag exists. The current sealer's generic raw-path format needs an explicit missing-bag/archive representation before it can consume that export. Do not manufacture a bag hash for an absent recording or retrospectively relabel development data.
