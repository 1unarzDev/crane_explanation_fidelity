# Numerical-usefulness development qualification

The new public interface defines quantitative odometry usefulness at 0.1 m disclosure resolution or a supported bound/range no wider than 0.1 m. It preserves the sampled source/window and makes no physical-motion or arrival threshold. Earlier methods, evaluator versions and results remain immutable under their own measurement rules. This is development on permanently exposed episodes, not confirmation.

The v4 authored screen completed **96 valid returns and 96 exact component agreements** across 48 contrasts on six exposed v13 packets. It used independent isolated A/B calls with opaque shuffled IDs. The threshold required complete agreement and valid returns; no call was retried or adjudicated. Terminal source and output hashes are verified and indexed in `development_motion_screen_v1/archive_manifest.json`. This is agent-assessed agreement with authored fixtures, not human validation or proof of exhaustive claim detection.

The separate equal-interface method run completed **72 valid unique attempts**: 36 deterministic HX-CONTRACT outputs and 36 strengthened HX-PROMPT calls, expanding to 108 aliased battery cells across six development episodes. Its transport threshold passed. Both methods received identical evidence packet bytes with the new public requirements. The baseline also received those requirements explicitly outside the untrusted-data block; the strengthened prompt itself remained byte-identical at SHA-256 `70ddce4c451f20b466d97d86e20473f7e8ca48acd57ee718f63d6917fb81d795`. Declared source and raw-output hashes are verified, with a terminal archive in `development_unique_methods_v2/archive_manifest.json`. This does not establish semantic superiority or qualification of a future provider.

`development_unique_scoring_v2` separately declares blinded A/B assessment of all 72 retained outputs, using the v4 judge and public packet-only references. It shares one disposition across identical request aliases. One independent blinded C is allowed only for component disagreement; previous votes and method identity are absent from C inputs. Unresolved outputs remain retained under least-favorable episode mapping. The run completed all 144 initial judgments with exact A/B component agreement, zero C calls and zero unresolved outputs. Both methods passed all six development episodes: zero favorable, zero reverse, six both-success and zero both-failure pairs. No p-value was computed. This tied result is preserved as development evidence; neither endpoint nor baseline is altered to create separation.

`motion_policy_admission.py` now requires matching hash-bound policy objects in battery, endpoint and comparator manifests. Admission demands complete operational evidence for equal requirements/interfaces, absent-motion handling, coarse/fine quantity distinctions, literal exactness, source/window scope, absence of physical thresholds and whole-episode alias/missingness handling. A scoped authored screen cannot satisfy that final gate alone. Full endpoint/runtime qualification, stable provider/model/version/decoding, N and the complete H1→H2 scientific freeze remain unfinished.

The separate secondary report records 100% useful-supported answers and complete required-unit coverage for both methods, with 0% unsupported-material flags. Mean answer length was 34.3 words for HX-CONTRACT versus 52.9 for HX-PROMPT; contract wording remains constrained and less flexible. Word-limit excesses are retained, not retried or excluded. These are 36 unique requests per method clustered in six exposed episodes, not 36 independent observations. They are agent-assessed descriptive results under the new public numeric interface, not a replacement for the original twelve-recording development result or an effect-size guarantee for confirmation.
