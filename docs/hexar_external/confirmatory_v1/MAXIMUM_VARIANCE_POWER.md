# Maximum-discordance effect-floor sensitivity

This supplemental prospective planning report reads no episodes, answers or labels and chooses no N or effect floor. It uses the already adopted uniform paired mixture, exact rational rejection thresholds and exact homogeneous binomial power, with independent six-family simulation as a cross-check. It changes neither the test nor the endpoint.

The earlier homogeneous .50/.80 example targets a **30-point whole-episode risk difference**, not the historical +9.3-point answer-level development result. Larger sample sizes reported under .50 discordance also cannot be treated as guarantees for every positive effect. The new report stresses total discordance 1.0 at primary mapped effects .05, .10 and .20, including fixed opposed-family effects with the same balanced overall mean. This maximizes discordance and stresses variance; it is not a proof that these are universally least-favorable power distributions or a realistic effect prediction.

For a .10 mapped effect and homogeneous discordance 1.0 (conditional favorable probability .55), exact power is .4185 at N=1,296, .7013 at 1,920 and .9489 at 3,072. On the sampled grid, the first homogeneous point exceeding .90 is 12,288 for .05, 3,072 for .10 and 768 for .20. These are grid points, not minimal-N decisions, final cohort sizes or extrapolations of the inspected 7:0 split. The `unequal_positive` row name denotes the template at .10; shifting it to .05 produces two mildly negative family effects, retained explicitly in the report. Opposed families remain sensitivity, without additional confirmatory claims.

Power targets must distinguish the **complete-label effect** from the **least-favorable mapped primary effect** after missing methods/judgments. An outcome-dependent unknown budget can consume the complete-label effect. For example, a .10 complete-label effect with .05 unknown per method can have zero mapped effect; increasing N cannot supply a positive mapped-effect guarantee. The prior unknown-dependence reports remain necessary. Qualification must inform a credible unknown budget, and final N must be justified against explicit accepted assumptions rather than selected from whichever power row is favorable.

Source/report pins: `maximum_variance_power.py` and `maximum_variance_power_sensitivity_v1.json`. The report contains 90 balanced family/effect/N scenarios, homogeneous null-size checks, simulation uncertainty and original source hashes. The active sample-size decision remains unbound pending complete acquisition/measurement qualification and effect-floor/unknown-budget selection.
