# Operational technical-validity candidate

This is an unbound prospective acquisition predicate, not permission to collect confirmation. Its implementation combines `technical_predicate.py` and `operational_validity_candidate.py` with independently recomputed raw/source/image/freshness checks. No method answer, judge label, expected winner, navigation success, command absence or covariance magnitude enters validity. All permitted adverse navigation outcomes remain included.

Require a unique planned episode identity/seed; matching captured provenance; a fresh offline container at the pinned image and domain; byte-verified captured artifacts and source-bank identity/closure; applied simulator seed evidence; measured reset; actual family setup; accepted navigation goal; raw topic/schema/count/integrity checks; coherent receipt/simulated/odometry clocks; and matching, ordered observed action boundaries. Require deterministic seeded start/goal/heading reproduction against the captured sampler and actual map. Reproduce both evidence extractions from raw bags. Recompute all nine public packet/reference/mask projections; aliases are identical within each query. Hidden metadata stays outside method/judge packets.

Required whole-bag channels must be present as specified by the recorded schema and interface. Missing optional diagnostic channels remain unknown. The retained task-window observations must lie inside the observed simulated-clock envelope, with no clock conversion, timestamp shifting or tolerance. Full-stream pre/post-task envelope violations remain reported but do not exclude an otherwise intact task-window interface. The [native clock amendment](NATIVE_CLOCK_VALIDITY.md) records the motivating development audit failure.

The v2 native qualification's at-least-two task-window samples is a qualification probe, **not an inclusion criterion**. A legitimately early action can contain fewer than two such samples. In that case the existing evidence interface marks odometry unavailable and requires no motion unit; it must still preserve useful software outcome communication. `operational_validity_candidate.py` deliberately does not exclude that episode or require a minimum action duration. Whole-bag stamped odometry and valid boundaries remain mandatory. This prevents technical selection from removing short or adverse navigation results.

Native and interface reviews must identify the same episode and exact action window. Boolean check reports are not self-authenticating: the final adapter must reverify receipt/raw/source hashes, recompute both native and interface reviews, and bind the rule and complete dependency closure. Final `technical_validity.json` remains unbound until that adapter is qualified. Development qualification must retain all failures under original identities, with no substitution or episode reissue.

For confirmation, selection will be the first prospectively required number passing this final frozen predicate within each family's ordered attempt schedule, before any explanation generation. N and the finite reserve remain unresolved. Technical-invalidity reasons and every attempted episode must be reported. Exhaustion prohibits enlarging N or reserves and precludes a confirmatory claim under the declared complete-cohort procedure. Semantic output/judge failures never authorize episode replacement.
