# Missing-bag raw archive and selection candidate

The previous generic sealer assumes every attempted recording has an existing raw file. A failed launch may have no bag, so that representation cannot retain every genuine invalid attempt without inventing a recording hash. Separate `raw_archive_v1.py` and `seal_archived_attempts_v2.py` candidates address this without changing the existing sealer or historical artifacts.

Every attempt receives an exclusive raw capture inventory manifest. Its SHA-256 identifies that inventory, **not a robot recording**. Real bag segment hashes are listed separately; absent bags remain an empty list with `bag_missing=true`. Failed SQLite sidecars or unfinished MCAP files can be retained as original artifacts without being promoted to complete recordings. Raw bytes, original capture provenance, prelaunch intent and driver phase/binding are verified, and development metadata cannot be relabeled. The manifest lives outside the immutable capture folder; derived evidence/review artifacts must use separate directories.

The selection candidate checks frozen allocated prefixes, both identity and seed exclusions, actual original/development bag hashes, archive/capture/raw-byte integrity, duplicate recording content across attempts, native validity-receipt hashes and the same freeze/predicate/attempt binding. Missing-bag attempts may receive explicit outcome-blind invalidity and consume a finite allocated reserve. They cannot be marked valid. Every selected valid episode needs an actual bag, measured independent reset and all required original intent/driver/controller/metadata artifacts. All attempts and technical reasons remain reported; any attempt after a family completes its valid quota fails. Reserve exhaustion produces no balanced cohort.

Twenty-one synthetic engineering cases cover missing-bag preservation, valid/reset requirements, development provenance/content exclusion, cross-attempt duplicate content, changed bytes, semantic contamination, validity binding, exclusive creation and finite ordered selection. No robot, model, judge, semantic endpoint or significance test is called. Hashes and packaging alone do not prove freshness or native technical validity.

**Production integration remains open.** A final wrapper must independently validate the committed prospective acquisition/scientific/family freeze, exact runtime/source/image/root and current exposure inventory, generate actual raw-confirmation provenance before launch, reproduce the frozen native checks, export validity receipts, and write a separate immutable cohort seal. These candidate functions do not authorize acquisition or manufacture a qualified predicate. No real confirmation cohort or data is created by this work.
