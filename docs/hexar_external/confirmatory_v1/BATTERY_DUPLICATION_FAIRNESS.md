# Identical-packet duplication and strong-baseline fairness

Prospective design candidate, before final N, methods or endpoint freeze. Existing development outputs and labels are unchanged. This rule is proposed independently of the pending complete v11 development scoring result.

The intact and irrelevant-removal conditions produce byte-identical legitimate packets for each query. The development executor generated another stochastic HX-PROMPT answer for each identical packet, while deterministic HX-CONTRACT reproduces its output. A strict any-failure endpoint across duplicate stochastic generations adds failure opportunities to the prompt method even though no new information or query is tested. Repeated-request reliability can be a legitimate estimand, but it should not silently become the headline prior-work comparison.

The proposed fair primary policy retains the nine-cell evidence battery but generates exactly once per distinct method request within each independent episode. A request includes the complete visible packet, exact query, prompt/model/decoding/system/context/tools/parser and transitive implementation/configuration binding. Byte-identical intact/irrelevant cells share one raw output or technical-failure disposition. Their scoring payload is consequently identical and can share one A/B(+C) assessment; no additional generation or adjudication is allowed merely because an alias failed. Different queries, evidence, methods, settings or episodes do not merge. Deduplication is computed and hash-bound before semantic generation, independent of outputs. Cross-episode caching is forbidden.

The current v12 packet audit demonstrates 108 battery cells but 72 unique method requests across six episodes, or six unique requests per method/episode. The independent statistical N remains six development episodes, never 72 or 108. Under this policy, the nine-cell any-failure endpoint equals failure on its six distinct query/evidence requests. Future power planning must use this effective battery; neither old nine-generation effects nor the answer-level +9.3 points is its prospective episode effect. Report unique-request secondary rates and clarify any duplicated-cell summaries instead of implicitly double-weighting intact evidence.

`unique_request_plan.py` is a pure fail-closed constructor with no provider calls or confirmation admission. Its implementation-binding field is not yet qualified; full runtime closure and exactly-once alias resolution still need admission tests. Adoption requires explicitly amending candidate battery/comparator/endpoint/execution artifacts and final power decisions before scientific freeze. A sealed plan must bind all aliases and unique request hashes. Existing 12-recording and later development results remain valid under their original, separately labeled development procedures; they are not re-scored into confirmation or substituted for the new generation design.

The admission audit now requires byte-equal policies in the battery, endpoint and comparator manifests, pinned constructor and alias-expansion code, and a pinned terminal qualification of the actual final execution adapter. The `hexar-unique-adapter-qualification/v1` report must bind those implementations and the scientific source, and pass all ten checks declared in `unique_admission.py`: complete request identity, single dispatch, shared failure without retry, isolation across episodes/methods/different requests, shared closed labels, all nine endpoint cells, single cost accounting and crash claims without reissue. Development fake-provider tests do not qualify a different future adapter. The qualification pin remains null and admission fails closed; this does not restrict development.

The analyzer additionally requires a hash-bound `unique_request_plan` in the terminal bundle, marked `SEALED_FOR_CONFIRMATORY_GENERATION`, with the matching `binding_freeze_sha256` and qualified implementation binding. Its alias set must exactly cover the frozen cohort/method/query/condition schedule and contain six distinct requests per episode/method. Every expanded job names its source unique request. Aliases must share raw-output hash, scoring-artifact hash and all three endpoint components, including explicit null missingness. Missing, duplicate, relabeled or inconsistent rows fail before the primary test. `unique_analysis_binding.py` is included in the adapter qualification bindings. These checks do not themselves authorize or qualify an actual confirmation adapter.
