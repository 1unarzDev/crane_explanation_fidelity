# Fixed development acquisition reliability qualification

Declared before the v16 campaign. This is development/engineering qualification, not semantic confirmation; all planned identities and seeds are permanently excluded from confirmatory N. No method or evaluator calls occur. The baseline, endpoint and alpha ledger are unchanged.

The campaign attempts exactly 24 independently seeded episodes in each of the six candidate simulated families, 144 attempts total, with no reserve, replacement or reissue. Interleave families by episode ordinal to distribute host/time effects. Use the current pinned TIAGo/Humble/Gazebo/Nav2 image, a fresh offline container for each attempt, domain 70, and the measured reset/family setup described in the v15 qualification. Bind a captured source bank before execution. The master seed is generated independently before acquisition; reject any identity/seed collision against every development plan before launching. N=144 here is an engineering qualification schedule, not the external confirmation sample size.

Run every allocated attempt, including after a technical failure; do not stop on navigation or explanation outcomes. Existing bounded per-attempt timeouts remain. Preserve each failure, raw receipt and attempted identity; unavailable artifacts produce explicit technical invalidity or unresolved qualification, never silently omitted rows. An interrupted host run must preserve its journal/receipts and cannot rerun an already attempted episode. No semantic output is generated from any of these episodes.

Before declaring acquisition-process qualification, complete raw/source/image/freshness checks and native seeded-sampling, clock, schema, count and extraction reproduction. Recompute the nine-cell evidence/reference interface. Apply the documented operational candidate without selecting on navigation success, action duration, covariance magnitude, command absence or missing optional diagnostics. The full-stream clock tail remains a diagnostic; retained task-window evidence must meet the exact clock-envelope rules. The native scope screen's two-sample probe is not an exclusion requirement for legitimately short actions.

The predeclared strong reliability criterion is that all 144 attempts close, all have complete independently reproducible technical dispositions, and all 24 attempts in every family are technically valid under the same candidate process. This is deliberately stricter than the later finite-reserve selection policy. If any family fails this criterion, retain the failed qualification and investigate in a separately declared development version; do not replace failures to manufacture a pass.

For planning, report family-specific observed technical-invalidity rates and exact binomial upper bounds, with a simultaneous 95% descriptive planning bound using .05/6 per family under independent, identically generated within-family attempts. With zero failures in 24, the upper bound is `1-(.05/6)^(1/24)` (approximately .181), below the candidate .20 invalidity ceiling. This is an engineering reserve-planning bound, not a semantic superiority test or a project discovery claim, and spends no confirmatory alpha. Its assumptions and limits must be reported: a small qualification cohort cannot prove an arbitrary rate guarantee or zero invalidity, and provider/evaluator failures are separate. Larger invalidity sensitivity remains in power/reserve planning.

Passing this campaign alone does not activate confirmation. The final adapter/runtime closure, operational evaluation qualification, stable provider binding, chosen effect assumptions/N/reserve and complete prospective H1/H2 family freeze remain necessary. Raw confirmation acquisition must occur under that later committed acquisition freeze with new identities/seeds.
