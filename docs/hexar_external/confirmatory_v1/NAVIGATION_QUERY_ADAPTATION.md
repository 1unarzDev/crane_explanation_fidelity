# Development navigation-only query candidate

The no-arm TIAGo/Nav2 harness executes navigation, not item delivery or manipulation. The inherited historical query battery includes delivery requests and questions presupposing failure or slowness. Those words remain preserved in the historical and existing candidate artifacts. They cannot establish that the new simulation executed the corresponding task.

`acquisition.navigation_context.project` supplies a separate development-only query candidate, identical across all six families:

- q1: What happened during the navigation task?
- q2: What does the available evidence show about the robot's navigation or motion?
- q3: Why did navigation proceed or end this way, and what remains uncertain?

The questions retain outcome, observation and causal-explanation functions while removing unsupported delivery and adverse-outcome presuppositions. They do not reveal the hidden family or intended intervention. Both methods receive identical wording, visible evidence and genuine public controller source. HX-PROMPT text remains unchanged. No methods or evaluators have been called using this candidate.

Projection preserves the actual recorded task/status bytes, including unexpected success, and qualifies their interpretation. A reported kitchen label does not establish a semantic zone; no item delivery is inferred. Software success is not upgraded to measured motion or physical goal attainment. When legitimate measured motion is supplied, both status and measurements must remain useful information; a visible discrepancy cannot be omitted merely to secure a coverage pass.

This candidate does not yet supply or qualify raw motion/goal evidence, references, contract realization, scoring, or whole-recording mandatory units. The final battery remains unbound pending that qualification. Freeze must bind the chosen questions, universal pre-derivation masks, public source and complete implementations. A change after confirmation cannot be justified from semantic outcomes.

The separate `navigation_packets.build` integration now produces 54 development packets from the six corrected-image v8 native event streams: three identical navigation questions × three evidence conditions per episode. It joins unchanged callback replay, genuine public controller source and the recording-scoped native motion projection. Intact and irrelevant-removal packets match; diagnostic removal preserves reported navigation outcomes while removing motion/command summaries before derivation. Hashes and full packets are retained in `navigation_packet_qualification_v1.json`. No method or judge was called. This demonstrates evidence/interface assembly, not contract coverage, reference correctness or scientific qualification.

Subsequent v11/v12 method and scoring development used these neutral questions, including actual controller-boundary evidence. The v13 offline interface audit independently assembled all 54 cells. The unbound candidate battery now adopts the implemented navigation queries identically across families and hash-binds their source; original per-family HEXAR wording remains archived in `historical_questions_by_family`. Earlier statements of no method/judge calls describe those earlier assembly stages, not the later completed development runs. No final scientific freeze is asserted.
