# Method-blind development inventory review v2

Finalized 2026-09-30 before qualified support scoring. The completed inventory is `data/hexar_external/v2/development/annotation/atomic_inventory.json`; the auditable manual construction helper is `developer_inventory_build.py` in the same directory. Only the blinded answer bank was read for this task. No method join key, production ontology, response cache, model console or reserved output was inspected. Independent reference rules, endpoint, production method and judge were not changed.

This is **method-blind developer inventory, not qualified model atomizer**. It is an automated research-agent extraction route, not human validation. The 162 response texts contain 96 unique exact texts; every unique text received a complete sentence-by-sentence semantic decomposition, and every repeated answer received its own opaque response ID and atom IDs. The artifact has 727 atoms over all answers, representing 531 atoms across the 96 unique texts. Reusing exact text decomposition is an extraction convenience, not additional physical or annotator independence.

## Completeness and mechanical audit

Every one of the 162 blinded IDs appears exactly once, with SHA-256 of the exact raw UTF-8 response text, without JSON encoding or normalization. All 727 atom IDs are unique. Every span is an exact substring of its unchanged answer. Every source sentence has at least one inventory atom anchored to that full contextual sentence; the union covers all non-whitespace response content. All 96 unique answer specifications are explicit in the developer helper, with no fallback text parser that silently drops an unknown answer. The helper checks sentence/specification counts before exporting.

Spans intentionally retain complete source sentences and overlap when a sentence expresses several propositions. The statement field isolates a single proposition, while the span preserves pronoun antecedents, epistemic qualifiers, causal attachment and timing context. These broad exact anchors are not separate empirical support and should not cause a judge to conflate sibling propositions. Qualified assessment must evaluate the isolated statement with the complete answer and permitted packet.

The inventory separates goal receipt, pending execution, recorded success/failure, timeout/abort, requested task and delivery claims, destinations, help requests, tray placement requests, later steps waiting, planner and controller reports, invalid/empty paths, path handoffs and costmap clearing requests. Local and global requests are separate where explicitly named. Reported or logged observations keep attribution; a claimed actual trip or physical arrival is not silently rewritten into a log-only fact.

Explicit “because” and “so” links are separate atoms from their antecedents and outcomes. Task noncompletion and failure to bring/fetch the book remain explicit rather than being discarded as style. Claimed consequences of waiting or aborted/timed-out navigation are preserved. Manual mode, plugged-in state and charging are separated where appropriate from alleged inhibited navigation or prevented delivery. Speed limitation and its asserted safety purpose are distinct. Path finding around obstacles is separated from retry and its claimed longer-time consequence.

Numerical and temporal facts are retained as assertions: approximate 2/16-second goal durations, a 37-second gap, approximate 10/15-second trips, approximately 70/72-second task windows, action return code 6 and goal coordinates (3.07, −0.34). Their accuracy was not decided by extraction. Waiting “was” and waiting “am” preserve tense. “Then,” “before,” “later,” first/second leg and event-before-success relations are separate temporal facts where meaningful. Requests to place a book on a tray are not converted into completed physical placement.

All uncertainty clauses retain their scope: unknown physical cause, unknown measured reason for navigation time, missing motion/speed/map/control evidence, unknown indicator contribution, unknown obstacle involvement and inability to identify perceived bad/slow movement. “Records do not establish X” remains a denial of evidential establishment, not a definite assertion that X did not occur. “May have delayed” and “could have slowed” retain modal possibility; they are not strengthened into demonstrated causes. No support, contradiction, physical-truth, endpoint, materiality or method labels were assigned.

## Scope choices and remaining uncertainty

The requested coarse levels are semantic indices, not a qualified diagnostic-rank or endpoint-role map. `software_action_failure` also indexes software events, reported localization trouble, measured-duration assertions and bounded software-delay relations. `recorded_override_state` covers manual/charging/plugged-in state. `specific_physical_cause` indexes asserted obstacle mechanisms, inhibited motion due to manual/charging and speed-limitation purpose. `limitation` indexes knowledge and evidence-availability assertions. Later assessment must retain source, polarity and modality rather than assume support from a level name.

Some answers give implicit task consequences or room arrivals beyond mere success status; those claims were kept so they can be judged against the full packet. “Had trouble determining position” is a qualitative diagnostic claim; it was not upgraded to a unique localization-caused failure. “Cannot tell whether movement was slow” does not assert normal speed. Subjective question premises are not independent episode evidence. Numerical durations may be affected by upstream timestamp conversion; retaining their atoms does not validate those measurements.

A single developer reviewer and repeated wording can induce correlated omissions or scope errors. Full-sentence anchors may increase attachment ambiguity. Counting complete sentence coverage is a mechanical coverage check, not a proof of semantic exhaustiveness or a qualified atomizer score. The parent independently audited all raw hashes/spans/IDs and substantive token coverage and reviewed a blinded 12-answer sample; that is project review, not human validation or held-out atomizer qualification. Qualified support assessors should see complete answers as well as atoms and retain any detected omission or unresolved scope rather than silently promote it.

The inventory is final for the declared development support pass. It must remain immutable once scoring launches. Corrections discovered afterward require versioned measurement provenance, retention of prior atoms/labels and affected-response or whole-recording sensitivity; they cannot become quality retries. The inventory itself supplies neither endpoint labels nor a treatment benefit.
