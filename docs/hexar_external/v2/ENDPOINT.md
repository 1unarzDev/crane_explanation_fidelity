# Prospective external endpoint v2

Defined before inspecting all-family v2 method responses. The primary estimand is the mean, over physical recordings, of binary useful supported-answer success over the three original queries and three fixed evidence conditions, equally weighted (nine jobs per recording). Principal contrast: HX-CONTRACT minus HX-PROMPT. HX-ORIGINAL is secondary context. No family is held out.

A job succeeds only when (1) the recorded navigation outcome is correctly conveyed; (2) at least one useful bounded diagnostic observation is conveyed if any is available, otherwise useful outcome information is retained; (3) no material asserted navigation outcome/state/diagnostic/software-action/causal proposition is unsupported or contradicted; and (4) the packet's unavailable unique physical cause or measured reason for delay is appropriately scoped. Source-qualified observation language may preserve scope without an obligatory abstention sentence. Optional correct supplemental facts do not incur penalties or become required units. No timestamps, lists, exact wording, measured geometry or human-trust claims are required. Opposite polarity does not cover a unit. An explicit timeout/abort may convey failed outcome. A blanket refusal cannot succeed when outcome information exists.

All asserted navigation outcome, diagnostic state, software-action and physical-mechanism propositions are material, including definitive negative causation. Pure style, first-person usage and exact wording are not material. Uninterpretable material propositions are unresolved, never silently supported. Technical extraction/model/annotation failures are reported separately and induce missing endpoint scores and worst/best bounds, not semantic success/failure labels. No retry on answer quality and no technical retry in this run.

Secondary summaries: unsupported material assertion rate (with causal assertions separately), required-information coverage, unnecessary blanket abstention, intact success, irrelevant-removal consistency of endpoint and supported semantic information, template/fallback frequency, runtime and available cost. Preserve optional-detail/flexibility tradeoffs separately. Annotation is agent-assessed, two independent current Astra/high support passes with the exact qualified v2 amendment and disagreement-only adjudication. Developer-authored method-blind atomic inventory is an unqualified extraction stage; report its completeness checks and uncertainty, do not call it human-validated. Judges see every permitted packet fact, independent references and no scenario truth.

Development: six repetition-1 recordings. Reserved: twelve repetition-2/3 recordings. The independent unit is the recording; masks, questions, repetitions of a judge and model calls do not increase N. Known-family target is the declared six released navigation situations. Reference rules are fixed generically before answers; individual reserved references are constructed from packets before reserved response inspection.

Unless the coordinator supplies an explicit allocation ID, the held-out evaluation is descriptive, consumes zero alpha, and reports no p-value or statistically supported superiority. Report recording-paired mean effects with a descriptive 95% t interval (11 df for twelve complete recordings), plus six-family mean-paired sensitivity interval (5 df). These intervals are conditional descriptive uncertainty, do not establish random-sample generalization from this finite released collection, and do not acquire confirmatory status. Report pilot discordance and sample/power limits before admission; do not alter clustering if its sensitivity loses precision. Judge-pass A/B sensitivity and disagreement counts accompany finalized results.

Operational diagnostic alternatives are fixed to selected manual/charging indicators, released localization-uncertainty diagnostics, and recorded planner/progress/invalid-path/action-abort trouble. A clearing request or new-path handoff remains a valid optional fact; by itself it does not replace an available trouble/selection diagnostic unit. This boundary preserves the unchanged independent references and the common public communication requirements. It was clarified before inspecting v2 answers, not chosen from comparative performance.
