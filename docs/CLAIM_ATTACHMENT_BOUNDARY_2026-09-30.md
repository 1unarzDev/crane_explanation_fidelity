## What still needs a validated mapping

The ontology is a diagnostic contract catalog, not an exhaustive language-to-endpoint map.
Its contracts do not encode proposition endorsement, modality or assertion polarity. It has
non-entailment relations and limitation text but no standalone `LIMITATION` claim contracts.
These structural observations do not prove that an arbitrary sentence matches an existing
contract or supply requirements for a negative proposition.

For example, “recovery did not cause success” asserts a causal negative; “recovery does not
establish success” expresses an evidential limitation. They cannot share a mapping merely because
both mention recovery and success. “The source threshold is 0.04 m/s” is a configuration fact;
“the episode exceeded that threshold” asserts an event. “Recovery ran” does not assert measured
response recovery. Quoted, hypothetical and unendorsed material must retain its scope; hedging
does not mechanically remove an endorsed episode assertion. Bare affirmatives and deictic
limitations require the exact question and full answer context, with unresolved referents
preserved rather than invented.

Before endpoint export, prospectively validate proposition-specific attachment, mechanistic
status, treatment of unmatched/negative/modal claims, family-to-response abstraction mapping
and unresolved-label policy. Do not borrow a nearby positive contract for an unmatched sentence,
count a limitation as a deeper causal assertion, or suppress an unresolved atom. Qualified
support/adjudication and an authorized join remain separate prerequisites. The existing analyzer
accepts resolved inputs; this inventory does not make retained inputs eligible for it.
