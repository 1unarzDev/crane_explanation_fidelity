# Land command--motion physical cohort

Status: **PHYSICAL CAPTURE FROZEN; SEMANTIC CAMPAIGN INACTIVE**  
Declared: 2026-09-24

This is a narrow prospective collection contract for independently checkable land
command-to-motion evidence. It does not repair the failed Luna endpoint-threat extension, activate
the registered semantic comparison, consume alpha, or change the frozen legacy study. The
machine-readable contract is
`research/explanation_fidelity/experiment_configs/prospective/land-command-motion-physical-cohort-v1.json`;
the exact 200-cluster schedule is `land-command-motion-physical-schedule-v1.json` in the same
directory.

## Why collect this cohort

Candidate-v3 development provides a useful bounded execution diagnosis, but its semantic endpoint
cannot be confirmed with the current failed Luna extension. Physical/reference collection remains
valuable because its central quantities are independently recomputable: delivered command,
measured motion, time windows, action result, and bounded execution sequence. Freezing the physical
population now prevents outcome-driven scenario selection while leaving semantic claims blocked
until a valid evaluator and activation amendment exist.

The target is land command--motion only. It is intentionally not the parent sequential protocol's
80/20 land/surface and 50/50 geometry/execution mixture. Results from this cohort therefore cannot
be inserted into that estimand. A future land-only semantic activation must be additive and must
bind its own target estimand before any P/R response is generated or inspected.

## Fixed population

The confirmation-side physical schedule has 100 independently configured clusters:

- 50 connected-detour and 50 nominal-clear-route geometries;
- 35 persistent discrepancies;
- 35 transient discrepancies with release after 12 seconds;
- 15 persistent discrepancies whose sole later method packet masks delivered odometry;
- 15 no-intervention nominal/false-premise controls.

The schedule excludes the already inspected runtime-qualification layout. A second disjoint set of
100 configurations has the same composition and remains an untouched replication reserve. The
schedule, family assignment, timing, collection order, run IDs, ROS domains, and ports are fixed by
a hash-pinned deterministic derivation. Replication collection is not authorized.

## Admission and retention

Each confirmation-side configuration is attempted once in fixed order. Exact player/build and
scenario binding, a valid zero-error Unity result, and retained robot-visible manifests are the
technical admission gates. Expected outcome, action success, diagnostic trigger, recovery, and
complete BT history are not admission gates. Valid unexpected and insufficient-evidence episodes
remain in the cohort. Invalid recordings remain in the ledger with their failed gate and are not
replaced.

Raw robot-visible captures retain commands, odometry, action state, plans, costmap observations,
and BT transitions when delivered. Evaluator geometry, intervention identity/timing, simulator
truth, and fault-induction expectation remain physically separate. The ambiguity mask is applied
only to a derived method packet; it does not erase the governed raw capture.

The independent reference uses the frozen low-speed one-second window computation. It can support
a delivered-command/measured-motion discrepancy or its absence. It cannot establish that an
actuator accepted the command, that Nav2 consumed delivered odometry, the evaluator intervention,
or a unique cause such as obstruction, collision, slip, or motor failure.

Before the first scheduled ambiguity run, amendment 1 replaced the historical paired-mask adapter
for this cohort only. The versioned v2 mask marks each scheduled ambiguity case as one independent
cluster, exposes only the masked export to future methods, and derives status and execution counts
from that cluster. Its independent v2 reference cannot inherit the old development case's abort or
attempt counts. Historical paired-mask files and results remain unchanged.

## Statistical boundary

No semantic P--R result, confidence sequence, guardrail, significance claim, or alpha expenditure
exists for this physical cohort. Before response generation, a future additive amendment must bind
the exact land-only estimand, P/R transport and schemas, independently constructed references, a
valid semantic endpoint, the tested sequential monitor, and the program alpha allocation. The
minimum worthwhile difference remains +0.15 and the existing material-error, coverage, ambiguity,
and fresh-replication requirements are not weakened.

If the confirmation-side cohort is closed early, only its fixed attempted prefix may be reported,
with the resource or validity reason. It cannot stop because intermediate physical or later method
outcomes look favorable.
