# Proposed marine material-assertion endpoint — development v1

This is a development instrument, not a qualified or frozen confirmatory primary endpoint. No old response bank is rescored. The sole proposed primary remains per-configuration-cluster mean useful/correct task-outcome explanation success across the fixed evidence-level questions, with equal weights over the predeclared paired variants. Ladder variants, answer generations and annotation passes add no independent configuration N. Allocation, resources, untouched populations, final endpoint/model/judge freeze and approved analysis remain necessary before activation.

A successful answer states the reported action outcome, supported physical task outcome at the declared temporal scope, decisive available measurements/interval evidence or evidence limitation, and necessary causal/evidence limits. When complete sampled kinematic/hull compliance is independently established, the answer must communicate that positive partial information even if full completion remains unknown because contact is missing. Blanket abstention does not satisfy this fifth conditional unit. The condition is selected by an independent reference before responses, identically for both methods, never by whether a baseline failed.

Material assertions concern the task outcome; motion, pose, contact, temporal or quantitative witnesses; governing configuration used in an explanation; evidence availability or temporal/sensing limits; or an explanatory physical cause. Negated cause assertions, statements of non-entailment, and unendorsed quotations retain their actual stance: “waves are not identified” does not endorse waves. A supported supplemental material assertion is permitted even when absent from the required-unit list. Extra numbers are not required for their own sake.

Only incidental background/courtesy or an unrelated unendorsed quotation may be excluded from primary support conjunction. An answer cannot exclude a claim about this episode, task requirements, evidence sufficiency, relevant configuration or offered cause merely by calling it optional. Every exclusion must have a method-blind pre-support relevance review, exact source span and declared reason. Unresolved relevance stays unresolved; no regex based on words like “wave” or “not” assigns stance or materiality. Generic source/citation assertions used to support the explanation are material; a truly unrelated background sentence can be incidental. Unsupported incidental content is retained in strict all-assertion sensitivity and secondary reporting, rather than erased.

The current qualified support pipeline labels every reviewed atom, all required units and both whole-answer limitations. The limitations separately check unidentified physical cause and missing-condition/temporal scope. They are assessed over the entire answer: an earlier disclaimer does not cancel a later contrary physical-completion, causal or proof assertion. A self-contradictory answer may communicate words from a required unit and still fail the material-support or limitation conjunction.

`analysis/roboboat_material_endpoint_v1.py` joins current atomic decision keys to a packet/answer-bound relevance review and computes the conjunction. It requires exactly both support-pass inventories, all four common units plus the independently applicable partial-compliance unit, and two whole-answer limitations. Missing transport/semantic judgments use explicit unknown (`None`) and retain conditional [0,1] score bounds; a definite supported scoring failure gives [0,0] even if other labels are missing. These are missing-judgment/relevance bounds, not confidence intervals and not physical task labels. Missing measurements in a robot packet require an **unknown physical outcome**, which can be a correct successful explanation. This is different from missing annotation of that explanation.

The instrument always marks semantic qualification and inferential authorization false and refuses an activation argument. Twenty-one construction-defined QA tests exercise unsupported material claims, supported extras, incidental strict-score sensitivity, answerable-information omission, whole-answer contradictions, uncertain labels/relevance and identity/inventory gates. These tests establish scoring arithmetic and structural contracts, not semantic reliability. The inherited global role/kind/polarity qualification does not authorize materiality mapping; this remains a separately reviewed/qualified gate. No human validation is claimed.

A fresh bounded contact/scope/coverage qualification successor must explicitly audit whole-response stance and evidence-sufficiency label boundaries before calls. Physical continuous compliance and availability of a continuous-time proof are different propositions: compliant samples alone leave physical continuous compliance unknown; an explicit packet declaration that no proof/bound exists contradicts a claim that such a proof is available. A point violation can establish physical failure. Gold labels must identify the proposition actually asserted, not treat lack of proof as a physical violation.

For future primary mapping, freeze the complete reviewed inventory/relevance procedure, treatment of hedged/unresolved propositions, conditional-unit selection, both whole-answer limitations and disagreement/adverse sensitivity before opening confirmation. The existing all-assertion analyses and post-inspection settling coverage sensitivity remain separate development results. No superiority follows from this proposal or the arithmetic tests.
