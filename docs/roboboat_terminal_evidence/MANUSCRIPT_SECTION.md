# Integration-ready marine development section

## Navigation success versus sustained docking

We apply the evidence-calibration question to an autonomous surface vessel: did a successful navigation action also establish the declared docking task? Marine relevance lies in measured motion after the action result and in the requirement to maintain pose, motion, hull and contact conditions over a specified interval. We reuse the existing simulator, validated navigation controller and docking environment. We do not introduce a new autonomy stack or a disturbance observer.

The public contract fixes a 0.40 m position tolerance, 0.35 rad heading tolerance, 0.05 m/s translation and 0.05 rad/s yaw-rate thresholds, an oriented hull/berth requirement, and prohibited-contact restrictions. Our separately declared development task uses a fixed five-second observed dwell beginning with the first post-result odometry sample, with eight seconds captured. This observation-aligned sampled contract does not replace the existing continuous docking evaluator. Point violations support failure; sparse or incomplete observations support unknown, and complete sampled compliance is reported with its temporal limitation. Continuous success requires additional justified intersample bounds. Missing contact telemetry cannot establish contact-free completion.

A historical development replay illustrates the distinction. The navigation action reported success with a result-adjacent measured position error of 0.3738 m and speed of 0.0486 m/s. The position margin to the independent bound was therefore 0.0262 m. A post-result sample at 239.040 s had position error 0.4004 m, beyond the 0.4000 m requirement within the declared dwell. The retained eight-second endpoint had error 0.5614 m. These observations establish the positional violation, while leaving its physical source unidentified. We retain actual result-receipt identity/time prospectively; the historical recording does not establish an exact action-return timestamp, and its first post-result sample is not relabeled as that event.

The same recording supplies nested removal-only evidence levels. With action status and the public contract alone, physical completion remains unestablished. Adding the result-adjacent pose and measured velocity permits an instantaneous assessment but not a dwell claim. Adding post-result trajectory and timing permits the bounded violation witness. Full recordings, endpoint summaries, derived diagnoses and evaluator labels are excluded from weaker packets, including tool-access paths. Body-frame measured velocity is rotated into the stable odom frame; commanded speed is not substituted for physical speed, and traveled distance is not added to radial pose error.

Fresh development collection prospectively declared three paired route configurations, with 0.20/0.40 m internal goal-checker tolerances under the unchanged independent requirement. The first pair passed capture validity; a direct-route recording reported navigation success but failed strict transport validity, stopping the queue. A bounded continuation then attempted the remaining untouched configurations. It retained a pre-player render-launch failure, corrected a demonstrated deadline-rounding defect, and used a separate successor identity without increasing physical N. All three subsequent physical captures passed strict validity. Five valid fresh recordings span three approach clusters, of which two contain complete tolerance pairs. They exhibit speed, position and yaw-rate violations during the fixed dwell despite successful navigation actions. No sustained-success recording was obtained. Live node/plugin parameter readback is available for the continuation; the original pair retains declared-launch provenance.

Independent arithmetic references agree with all retained certificates, and removal-only ladder audits pass. The strong tool agent receives the same public contract, observations, executable certificate and renderer source. Semantic assessment uses the current qualified two-pass blinded agent pipeline with a separately bounded eight-case marine temporal/contact qualification. These are agent-assessed labels, not human validation. Across the historical replay and five valid fresh recordings, 36 answers and 72 support passes finalized without disagreement. Both methods achieved 15/15 fresh answer success and 60/60 required-unit coverage under the conjunctive development score. The mean B4-minus-B2 difference across the two complete fresh paired clusters was zero. The tie is retained; this small inspected sample provides no confirmatory comparative interval or adequate power estimate. The baseline supplied additional supported detail that the required-unit endpoint does not fully measure.

One shifted-route speed crossing illustrates a renderer limitation: 0.050010012 m/s against 0.050000000 m/s is rounded to equal-looking values in the frozen four-decimal output. An independently versioned development correction preserves the printed inequality without replacing the comparative outputs. Perfect agent-assessed success therefore does not imply identical quantitative presentation or language utility. The deterministic method uses no model calls; the nine continuation strong-agent calls had median latency of 62.44 s. Dollar cost and separate certificate-computation latency are not measured.

## Contribution and limitations

Explainable ASV docking, contrastive maritime planner explanations, maritime numerical-to-language interfaces and explained LLM navigation are established directions. Our focused target is an auditable temporal completion certificate and a same-recording test of explanation sufficiency against an equally informed tool agent. The generic use of templates or runtime verification is not a novelty claim.

This development result establishes a useful contract/evidence boundary and a physically reproduced difference between action outcome and terminal dwell. It does not establish comparative superiority, broad marine population coverage, hardware transfer, continuous-time success, or wind/current/wave attribution. The original pilot stopped on a measurement-quality failure; its bounded continuation completed the registered remaining configurations. Limited independent diversity, absent fresh sustained-success capture and the development sentence-inventory endpoint still prevent confirmatory power planning or a meaningful comparative uncertainty claim. No boat confirmatory alpha was allocated, and no boat result is pooled with land evidence. A tie with the strong agent is a valid outcome; confirmation requires a separately coordinated allocation, prospectively frozen endpoint/population/analysis and untouched configurations.

A separately declared fixed development extension adds two stricter internal-stopping variants on existing approaches, with unchanged independent physical requirements. Both show sampled position, heading, velocity and hull compliance throughout the predeclared five-second dwell, while full task completion remains unknown because contact evidence is unavailable. The known-route position later exceeds 0.40 m at about 7.04 s into the longer capture. This later violation illustrates the need to state temporal scope: it does not falsify the earlier sampled dwell, nor does that dwell prove indefinite or continuous docking. These additional variants add no independent approach-cluster N. Their twelve method answers are retained; atomic support review is pending. A separate unpromoted renderer makes established component-level interval compliance explicit, addressing a utility limitation without changing the frozen comparison.

The complete retained core answer bank has also undergone two-pass atomic extraction and project completeness review. Thirty-five of thirty-six support packets finalized; one retained 300-second annotation timeout belongs to the already-unpaired direct-route variant. Explicit missing-judgment sensitivity therefore leaves the two complete original pairs tied, with conditional difference bounds [0,0]. Those bounds are not statistical confidence intervals, and shared judge errors remain possible. No available atomic support or required-unit disagreement was observed; unqualified abstraction tags are excluded. The original sentence-inventory scores remain separate, and the stricter atomic sensitivity does not promote the proposed material-assertion primary endpoint.

The fixed internal-stopping extension exposes a language tradeoff. The strong agent explicitly communicates both observed compliant sampled intervals, while the frozen deterministic renderer leaves those components implicit and emphasizes unknown full completion. An equal-method development coverage unit yields 2/2 versus 0/2 partial-compliance coverage; extended all-assertion success ties at 4/6. This coverage unit was declared after response inspection, so it cannot support confirmatory superiority. The inherited strict scores are 4/6 for the agent and 6/6 for the renderer, but the difference is driven by a public-contract ambiguity: `contact_required:true` does not explicitly define absence versus assessment, while the production computation treats prohibited contact as a violation. Both judges reject the agent's required-contact-absence assertions as insufficiently established by that packet. This defect requires a versioned contract repair and excludes interpreting the strict difference as demonstrated explanatory superiority. All old outputs and labels remain retained; no new contact observations or full-success claims follow.
