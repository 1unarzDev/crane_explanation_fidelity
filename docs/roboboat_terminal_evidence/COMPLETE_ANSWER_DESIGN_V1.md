# Complete supported diagnostic answers: development design

The user selected **complete supported diagnostic answers** as RoboBoat's primary endpoint. This document records development decisions, not a confirmation freeze. Confirmation and replication N remain zero.

For each question, success requires every independently answerable required information unit, preservation of evidence and temporal limitations, and no material unsupported assertion. Required units include reported navigation status, physical docking support at the public fixed dwell, a decisive observed violation and its time when available (otherwise the relevant evidence limitation), and withholding an unidentified physical cause. Full sampled kinematic/hull compliance must be communicated when answerable even if contact remains unknown. Supported supplementary details are allowed. Every atomic error remains in supporting reporting; a whole-answer materiality decision cannot hide unresolved atomic annotations.

Use the existing bounded-qualified material-v3 workflow as a development candidate. Extend reliability assessment to the broader population and new full-CRANE answers before final qualification. Source inventory review and evidence-support judgment remain distinct. Reviews and judges are agent-assisted, not human validation; same-family errors and disagreement need explicit reporting. No method receives evaluator references or annotation decisions.

The provisional estimand is the equal-weight average success difference across the six fixed questions (two tolerance variants × three evidence levels), averaged over independent geometry draws. One geometry is one cluster. Variants, masks, prompts, model families, repeats and source repairs add zero N. Preserve the predeclared family mixture; inspect heterogeneity without selecting families according to which method wins. Invalid physical captures are admitted or excluded using method-blind technical rules, with all attempts retained. Missing judgments require explicit adverse/favorable bounds, not complete-case deletion.

A ten percentage-point improvement is a provisional scientifically meaningful planning effect: approximately one additional complete supported answer per ten explanation requests. It is not an estimated development effect or a required observed significance threshold. Report sensitivities at five, ten, fifteen and twenty points, and reconsider operational usefulness before freezing, independently of which endpoint gives the largest observed effect. The paired superiority null is mean B4-minus-B2 success ≤ 0; the minimum meaningful effect drives sample planning.

The existing power sensitivity permits hundreds of fresh geometries. With hypothetical discordance .40 and maximal correlation of paired differences, a ten-point improvement requires approximately 410 valid configurations at one-sided alpha .025 and 90% power under the normal planning approximation. This is neither selected N nor validated exact test power. Whole-configuration invalidity, missing-annotation bounds and family heterogeneity must enter prospective simulations before choosing the final test/N. Reserve replication through further generation rather than reducing confirmation below adequate power. No ordinary repeated unadjusted significance checks govern collection.

## Full method and fair baseline

Historical marine certificate/renderer outputs remain development renderer benchmarks. `roboboat_full_crane_v1.py` now invokes the shared maximal diagnosis and realization/verification functions through static public marine contracts and numeric source bindings. Its current realization is deterministic with zero model calls. This satisfies an executable architecture integration milestone, not final scientific validation or an LLM-realization claim.

The existing sol/astra response screens had access to the marine certificate/renderer source closure. Preserve that strong method-reuse sensitivity without relabeling it retrospectively. A successor primary B2 must receive identical visible evidence, exact relevant robot source/configuration and all permitted primitive computations. Before its new calls, declare the source/tool interface and best qualified model setting. Do not silently remove a previously permitted diagnostic helper to manufacture an advantage. Explicitly report whether full B4-specific contracts/planning/verification are accessible to B2; if accessible, label the comparison as tool-enabled method-reuse sensitivity. If a primary architecture comparison uses only shared primitives, justify the boundary against the paper's method definitions before generating outputs, and retain the stronger reuse condition as supporting evidence.

No endpoint or method change after inspected confirmation may be justified by unfavorable results. All current data and changes are development only. RoboBoat and land inference remain separate.
