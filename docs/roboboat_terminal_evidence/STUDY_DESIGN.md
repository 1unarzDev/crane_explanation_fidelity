## Contract and temporal scope

`task_contract_v1.json` declares the same target and fixed independent position, heading, stopped-motion, hull, berth, and contact requirements for all methods. It prospectively evaluates a fixed five-second dwell beginning at the first post-result odometry observation, with eight seconds captured. This is an observation-aligned immediate dwell, not eventual settling or an outcome-selected interval. It does not replace the validated independent evaluator's continuous five-second contract. The legacy motivating case is replayed development evidence only.

A: action reported success. B: measured pose/motion at a stated observation. C: observed trajectory crossed or stayed within a bound. D: compound compliance over the declared dwell. E: identifiable physical cause. A does not imply D; C does not identify E. The method retains separate sampled and continuous support. A valid robust violation establishes false; missing observations establish unknown. True sampled compliance needs complete coverage within the fixed gap limit and every required component. Without justified intersample bounds, continuous success is unknown even when sampled compliance is true. An observed point violation also falsifies continuous compliance on an observation-aligned interval. Missing contact telemetry prevents a full success claim. Evaluator contact truth is never a method input.

Zero measurement uncertainty represents simulator numerical observations only. It is not hardware accuracy. Measured body surge/sway are rotated into the fixed odom frame; speed is their norm. Feedback and commanded speeds cannot substitute. Wrapped yaw uses the shortest angle. Hull containment uses a public oriented rectangular footprint and berth; precise collision geometry and contacts remain separate requirements.

## Evidence levels and methods

Same question at L0–L2: “Did the boat complete the specified docking task, and what evidence explains the difference, if any, from the navigation result?” L0 has status and public contract/configuration; L1 adds result-adjacent pose and measured velocity; L2 adds the post-result trajectory. L3 is activated only for actual robot-visible actuation/contact streams with a capture contract. Evaluator docking labels cannot supply L3. No endpoint, full-source path, images, answer keys, source hash lookup, or derived summary survives a removed stream. Histories allow finite-difference average motion; packets never say speed is impossible to estimate from retained poses.

Marine contract condition maps conceptually to B4 and strong structured evidence/tool agent to B2. Both receive the same packet, contract, source snapshot, and executable primitive/certificate calculations. The baseline may run scripts/templates, inspect exact relevant source/config, and reproduce the certificate. Labels are local marine development mappings, not changes to land implementations. Deterministic contract language is acceptable and explicitly disclosed. Optional instantaneous-only ablation is secondary and not yet activated.

## Pilot and population

`pilot_registry_v1.json` registers six rows in three paired approach clusters before capture. Known feasible route and direct far-goal route are retained. The third approach shifts the known free-water stem by 0.10 m within the documented 1.146 m route clearance; the terminal berth target stays fixed. Its fresh feasibility and outcome remain measured data. Pair order is AB/BA/AB, with a fresh player reset per row. The two internal tolerances are 0.20 and 0.40 m under the same independent 0.40 m task requirement. Seed differences are bookkeeping, not physical diversity. No outcome-dependent retry, replacement, or population selection is permitted. Start/route feasibility, heading/speed limitations, contact observability, and runtime throughput are measured rather than invented. Stop on a technical failure, retain it, and declare a successor attempt prospectively if repair is necessary.
