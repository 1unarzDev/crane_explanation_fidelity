# Fresh route population v2

`generate_roboboat_population_v2.py` creates independently sampled development geometry clusters using six predeclared families: aligned long/short terminal approach, curved stem, terminal S-bend, terminal dogleg, and direct NavigateToPose. It varies terminal straight-run length/direction and curvature while preserving the existing physical initial state, public berth, hull, controller, sensing and physics. The path anchor is an approximate scene-default coordinate, never described as physical start-pose variation. Static clearance and physical feasibility remain unasserted; technical admission is independent of navigation or explanation outcomes.

Input-design revisions are tangent-derived interior yaw, subdivision to at most .20 m waypoint chords, smooth Hermite stem/connectors, and namespace-specific launch seeds. The exact final pose always equals the public requested goal. Final requested orientation is distinct from route tangent and remains in the original ±.10 rad goal range. An input-only construction constraint reserves at least a one-metre connector north of the turn; it uses no execution outcomes. Physical berth geometry is not moved with perturbed goals.

Fresh registry `populations/development-42004/registry.json` contains24 geometry draws/48 tolerance variants, generated before execution. A geometry contributes at most one independent cluster. The .2/.4 m tolerances use balanced AB/BA order; variants/evidence levels/repeats add no N. No confirmation is frozen or executed. The existing42003 population remains available as an unchanged-v1 extension;42004 explicitly belongs to revised route-input designv2. Neither population is a sample ceiling or replication reserve.

Three tests pass, including a120-geometry construction exercise checking finite/wrapped headings, waypoint bounds, task/goal and berth consistency, reproducibility, unique cross-namespace launch seeds, two-variant cluster identity, and refusal to generate confirmation without a separate protocol. Empirical collection will establish which execution outcomes occur; success is not a validity requirement. Broader physical start-pose/heading variation still requires a separately bound platform initialization extension, as documented in SCENARIO_DIVERSITY_DESIGN_V2.md.
